Kerala road tax for electric vehicles

Last updated: November 2026

Kerala charges road tax on electric vehicles, but at lower rates than petrol or diesel cars. For a new electric car, the one-time lifetime road tax is 3% of the purchase value up to Rs.10 lakh, 5% above Rs.10 lakh up to Rs.20 lakh, 10% above Rs.20 lakh up to Rs.40 lakh, and 15% above Rs.40 lakh. That is the direct answer. There is no full exemption any more, and the old 50% discount has ended, so check which price band your car falls in before you compare quotations. The rates below come from the Kerala Finance (No. 3) Act, 2026, and match a calculator checked against the same slabs in September 2026. Treat the total on your own Vahan payment screen as final.

The current EV road tax slabs in Kerala

The tax is a one-time lifetime tax paid at first registration, calculated on the purchase value, which is the ex-showroom price of the variant you buy.

EV vs petrol: what the saving looks like

Petrol cars pay 10% below Rs.5 lakh, 13% up to Rs.10 lakh, 15% up to Rs.15 lakh, 17% up to Rs.20 lakh and 22% at Rs.20 lakh and above. Against those, the EV saving at registration is large:

How the tax is calculated and paid

  1. Confirm the ex-showroom price of your exact variant. Road tax is calculated on that price, and a manufacturer price revision changes the tax with it.
  2. Find your band in the table above. The rate applies to the full purchase value, not only to the part above the limit.
  3. Check the dealer quotation line by line. The dealer usually collects road tax with the registration charges and pays it at registration. Ask for road tax as its own line, and compare it with the table above.
  4. Pay through the portal where the option is shown. If you pay road tax yourself, use the taxation service on Parivahan or the Kerala MVD site, enter the vehicle number, and check the fee panel before paying. Our guide to paying Kerala road tax online walks through the Vahan steps.
  5. Keep the receipt. The receipt is the record of what was charged, and you will need it for any refund claim later, for example if the vehicle moves out of Kerala. See our road tax refund guide.

What changed, and when

Kerala's EV road tax has changed three times in six years, so older guides still quote rates that no longer apply.

  • 2019 policy, full exemption, then a 50% discount: the Kerala EV policy introduced in 2019 exempted eco-friendly vehicles from road tax and registration charges during the policy period, and the state later offered a 50% road tax discount for the first five years of ownership. Both have ended, which is why many older articles still say EVs pay half tax in Kerala.
  • April 2020 to March 2025, flat 5%: Kerala then charged a flat 5% road tax on all EVs.
  • April 2025, slab-wise: the 2025 state budget, effective from 1 April 2025, moved to price bands: 5% up to Rs.15 lakh, 8% from Rs.15 lakh to Rs.20 lakh, and 10% above Rs.20 lakh. Electric cars with a battery renting facility were taxed at a flat 10% regardless of price.
  • July 2026, current slabs: the Kerala Finance (No. 3) Act, 2026, published in the Kerala Gazette on 8 July 2026, cut the rate to 3% up to Rs.10 lakh, left Rs.20 lakh to Rs.40 lakh at 10%, and raised above Rs.40 lakh from 10% to 15%.

Small and mid-priced EVs got cheaper to register, while luxury EVs got dearer, yet registrations fell after the old discount ended. One report puts Kerala EV registrations at 75,812 in 2023 and 17,114 by September 2025. Treat that as reported context, not as an official count.

Electric two-wheelers and three-wheelers

No separate two-wheeler or three-wheeler EV slab could be confirmed in the 2026 Act excerpts read for this post, which cover electric motor cars, electric motor cabs, electric tourist motor cabs and electric private service vehicles for personal use. Older guides quote 5% for EV two-wheelers and three-wheelers regardless of price, but that figure could not be re-verified against a current official source in this run, so it is not quoted here as a current rate. If you are buying an electric scooter or an e-auto, ask the dealer for the road tax line on the quotation and check it against the fee panel on Vahan before you pay. No current e-auto purchase incentive could be verified either, so do not count on one.

What else you pay at registration

Road tax is the largest head, but not the only one. One of the others is usually nil for an EV.

  • Registration certificate fee: the central government exempted battery-operated vehicles from the fee for issue or renewal of the registration certificate and for the assignment of a new registration mark. In practice this line is usually nil for an EV, but confirm it on your Vahan receipt.
  • High security registration plate: EVs carry green number plates, fitted as an HSRP at the dealership. See our HSRP price guide for Kerala.
  • Insurance: a valid motor insurance policy is required at registration, priced separately from road tax.
  • Green tax: green tax is not charged on a new vehicle. It applies at re-registration after 15 years. See our green tax guide for the amounts that apply then.

Frequently asked questions

Are electric vehicles exempt from road tax in Kerala?

No, not any more. EVs were fully exempt during the early policy period and then carried a 50% discount, but both have ended.

What is the road tax on an electric car costing Rs.10 lakh?

Rs.30,000, which is 3% of Rs.10 lakh. A petrol car at the same price pays Rs.1,50,000.

What is the road tax on an electric car above Rs.40 lakh?

15% of the purchase value, under the Kerala Finance (No. 3) Act, 2026, up from 10% before July 2026.

Is the road tax a one-time payment or yearly?

For a private vehicle it is a one-time lifetime tax paid at first registration, not an annual charge. Commercial EVs follow the quarterly or annual pattern for their class, a different schedule.

Does the 3% rate apply to electric scooters?

That could not be verified in this run. The 2026 Act excerpts read here cover electric cars and cabs, and older guides quote 5% for EV two-wheelers without a current official source. Check the fee panel on Vahan for your scooter before you pay.

Do I pay road tax again if I move my EV to Kerala from another state?

Yes, Kerala levies road tax afresh when a vehicle from another state is re-registered here, and you claim a pro-rata refund of the old state's tax separately. See our guides to bringing a car from another state to Kerala and to road tax refunds.
