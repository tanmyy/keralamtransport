Change of address in RC in Kerala

Last updated: October 2026

To record a change of address in your RC in Kerala, apply in Form 33 through Vahan vehicle services on parivahan.gov.in, or at the RTO that covers your new address. The official fee is half the registration fee for your vehicle class under Rule 81 of the Central Motor Vehicle Rules: Rs.150 for a two-wheeler and Rs.300 for a private car, plus Rs.200 if a smart card RC is issued. Parivahan guidance says to apply within 14 days of the address change. Keep the original RC, proof of the new address, valid insurance and a valid PUC ready before you start.

Same RTO, different RTO, or another state: which case are you?

This is the first decision, because the three cases do not use the same paperwork.

Your moveWhat you file
You moved, but your new address is still under the same RTOForm 33 change of address only. Your KL number does not change.
You moved to an address under a different RTO inside KeralaForm 33 at the RTO for the new address. Your registration mark normally stays the same, but the registering authority record is updated.
You moved permanently to another state, or you are bringing a vehicle into Kerala from another stateThis is not a simple Form 33 case. You need a No Objection Certificate from the old RTO in Form 28, then assignment of a new registration mark in the new state in Form 27, with road tax assessed there. See our guide to NOC for car transfer from Kerala to another state.

Do not file Form 33 alone for an inter-state move. The vehicle record, tax record and registration mark all have to move.

The official fee

Parivahan's Fees and User Charges page, read in October 2026, lists change of residence at half the fee in the registration table, under Rule 59. Working that half-fee out class by class gives the table below.

Add Rs.200 if the RC issued is a smart card type. Parivahan notes that the fees in its table are exclusive of GST. Two cautions: older Kerala pages still quote a flat Rs.20 for this service, which does not match the current Rule 81 half-fee table, so do not rely on it; and if a No Objection Certificate is part of your case and its submission is delayed, Parivahan lists an additional Rs.300 per month of delay for motorcycles and Rs.500 per month for other vehicles. The amount shown on the Vahan fee panel for your own application is the final figure to pay.

Documents required

Parivahan's change of address page lists the following. Items marked with a star may be asked for in some states, so keep them ready even if the Kerala flow does not ask for every one.

  • Application in Form 33, filled and signed
  • Original certificate of registration (RC)
  • Proof of the new address, such as Aadhaar, passport, voter ID, or a utility bill or rent agreement in your name as accepted by the portal
  • Valid insurance certificate, ideally updated or endorsed with the new address
  • Valid Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate
  • No Objection Certificate or consent from the financier, if the vehicle is under hypothecation*
  • Smart card fee, where a smart card RC is issued*
  • Attested copy of PAN card, or Form 60 and Form 61 where applicable*
  • Pencil print of the chassis and engine numbers*
  • Signature identification of the owner*

Our change of address service page carries the short version of this checklist. If the vehicle is on a loan, start the financier consent early: the RTO cannot finish the entry cleanly while the hypothecation record points to the old papers, and the financier's own timeline is outside the RTO's control.

Steps: apply online on Vahan

  1. Open parivahan.gov.in and go to Online Services, then Vehicle Related Services.
  2. Select Kerala as the state and enter your vehicle registration number.
  3. Proceed to Basic Services and validate the record with the last five digits of the chassis number.
  4. Generate the OTP sent to the mobile number linked to the record, enter it, and submit.
  5. Select Change of Address.
  6. Enter the service details, including the full new address exactly as it appears on your address proof.
  7. Update the insurance details if the portal asks for them.
  8. Check the fee panel, then pay the amount shown online and save the receipt.
  9. Upload the documents if the portal enables uploads for your RTO, and book an appointment if it asks you to.
  10. Note the application number. The application then moves to the RTO for processing, and you track it through the application status service on the same portal.

Online or RTO visit? The application, payment and receipt are online. You visit the RTO only if the portal gives you an appointment or asks for the original RC and originals for verification. Carry the fee receipt, the original RC, and originals of everything you uploaded.

Steps: apply at the RTO

  1. Download Form 33 from Parivahan or collect it at the RTO, and fill it in full, including any hypothecation details.
  2. Attach the RC, new address proof, insurance and PUC, plus financier consent if the vehicle is under a loan.
  3. Submit the file at the RTO that covers your new address and pay the fee from the table above, plus the smart card charge where it applies.
  4. Keep the stamped acknowledgement or fee receipt. That number is how you follow the application.
  5. Collect or receive the updated RC as that RTO directs, and check that the new address is printed correctly before you leave the counter or accept delivery.

What to check after the RC is updated

  • The new address matches your address proof word for word, including PIN code.
  • The registration number, chassis and engine numbers have not picked up a typing error during re-entry.
  • Your insurance record shows the same new address. If it does not, get the policy endorsed separately, because a mismatch can slow a claim or a later RC transfer.
  • Your driving licence still shows the old address? That is a separate Sarathi service, not part of Form 33. See driving licence address change online in Kerala.

What could not be verified

Two gaps, stated plainly. First, no fixed Kerala processing timeline for a Form 33 change could be verified on an official page, so this post does not quote a number of days: track your own application number, because its stage is the reliable signal. Second, mvd.kerala.gov.in did not respond from here at research time in October 2026 (the direct check returned no connection), so the fee and document lists above rest on the Parivahan pages, which were read in full. If the Vahan fee panel shows a different total for your vehicle, pay the panel total and keep the receipt.

FAQs

What is the fee for change of address in RC in Kerala?

Half the registration fee for your class: Rs.150 for a two-wheeler and Rs.300 for a private car, plus Rs.200 if a smart card RC is issued. The full class table is above.

Which form is used for RC address change?

Form 33, under Rule 59 of the Central Motor Vehicle Rules and Section 49 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988. For an inter-state move you also need Form 28 for the NOC and Form 27 for a new registration mark.

How soon should I apply after moving?

Parivahan guidance says within 14 days of the address change. Apply promptly even if you have missed that window, and keep the acknowledgement.

Does my vehicle number change if I move within Kerala?

No. A move inside Kerala, even to a different RTO, updates the address and registering authority record. The number changes only when a vehicle is re-registered in another state.

Can I change the RC address without visiting the RTO?

You can file and pay online on Vahan. A visit is needed only if the portal books you an appointment or asks for originals. Whether your RTO completes the case without a visit is shown during your own application flow.

Do I also need to change the address on my driving licence?

Yes, separately. The RC and the driving licence are different records on different portals: Form 33 and Vahan for the vehicle, Sarathi for the licence.
