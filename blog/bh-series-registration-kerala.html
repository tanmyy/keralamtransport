BH series registration in Kerala

Last updated: October 2026

The direct answer: BH (Bharat) series registration is a voluntary, all-India mark for non-transport (private) vehicles only, available in Kerala if you are defence personnel, a Central or State government or PSU employee, or a private sector employee whose employer has offices in four or more states or union territories. A BH vehicle needs no new registration mark when you move state, and its tax is paid in two-year blocks instead of as one lifetime payment. The Kerala point most guides miss is tax: after a Kerala High Court ruling in January 2025, BH vehicles in Kerala are taxed under the Kerala Motor Vehicle Taxation Act, 1976, not at the flat central rates on most national pages, so the tax shown on the Vahan payment screen for your vehicle is the figure to go by.

What the BH number looks like

MoRTH introduced the BH series by notification dated 26 August 2021 (G.S.R. 594(E)). The mark is generated randomly through the portal, so you cannot choose the number.

Part of the markMeaning
YY (first two digits)Year of first registration, for example 26 for 2026
BHCode for the Bharat series
#### (four digits)Randomised number from 0000 to 9999
XX (two letters)Randomised alphabets from AA to ZZ

A plate reading 26 BH 1234 AB was first registered in 2026 under the Bharat series. It carries no Kerala district code, unlike a normal KL plate. The plate itself is a high security registration plate; our HSRP number plate Kerala price guide covers fitment in Kerala.

Who is eligible in Kerala

CategoryProof of eligibility
Defence personnelOfficial service identity card
Central or State government employeesOfficial identity card
Central or State PSU employeesOfficial identity card or employer certificate
Private sector employeesForm 60 working certificate from the employer, confirming the company has offices in four or more states or union territories, plus the employment identity card

If none of these categories fits you, you are not eligible, however often you travel. BH is also not available for transport vehicles such as taxis, goods vehicles or buses. Self-employed applicants, and employees of companies operating in fewer than four states, stay on the normal KL series. Eligibility is checked from the documents above, and weak employment proof is the common reason a BH request is not approved.

Road tax for BH vehicles in Kerala: read this before quoting any rate

Lead with the official Kerala position, because national pages quote a central table that does not set your Kerala bill. Rule 51B sets the principles: tax is levied for two years or a multiple of two, and after the fourteenth year annually at half the amount charged earlier. Rule 51B(2) also printed central rates of 8 percent of invoice price below Rs.10 lakh, 10 percent from Rs.10 to 20 lakh and 12 percent above Rs.20 lakh, with 2 percent extra for diesel and 2 percent less for electric vehicles.

In January 2025 the Kerala High Court, in Harish Kumar KP v Union of India (2025 LiveLaw (Ker) 13), held that prescribing the tax rate is within the states domain, that the central rates in Rule 51B(2) are not enforceable, and that BH vehicles should be registered with tax charged under the Kerala Motor Vehicle Taxation Act, 1976, following the principles in Rule 51B(1) and (3). In practice: the two-year block pattern applies, but the rate is the Kerala Act rate, and no separate Kerala BH rate table could be verified on parivahan.gov.in or mvd.kerala.gov.in in this run (mvd.kerala.gov.in did not respond at research time). Treat the Vahan payment screen total for your vehicle as final, and check any dealer quote against it before paying; a gap is normally a service charge or add-on, so ask for an itemised breakup.

No separate BH-specific registration fee could be verified on an official fee page in this run. Registration, smart card and plate charges, if any, appear in the same Vahan fee panel, which is the final figure.

Documents to keep ready

  • Form 20, the application for registration of a motor vehicle (filed on Vahan, usually through the dealer for a new vehicle)
  • Proof of eligibility: official identity card for government, PSU and defence applicants; Form 60 working certificate and employment identity card for private sector applicants
  • Identity and address proof, such as Aadhaar, passport or voter ID
  • Vehicle invoice or sale certificate from the dealer
  • Valid motor insurance certificate
  • Pollution Under Control certificate, where the portal asks for it
  • Passport-size photograph, if prompted

Step by step: apply for BH registration in Kerala

  1. Confirm eligibility first. Get the identity card or Form 60 certificate in hand before the application starts; eligibility is verified from these papers, not from a declaration alone.
  2. Start the new vehicle registration on Vahan. For a new vehicle the dealer normally files Form 20 on vahan.parivahan.gov.in for the Kerala RTO.
  3. Select the BH series as the registration type, instead of the normal state series, and enter the vehicle, invoice and owner details exactly as on the documents.
  4. Upload the eligibility and vehicle documents listed above, in the format the portal specifies.
  5. Pay the tax and fees shown on the Vahan screen. This is the first two-year tax block under the Kerala Act, plus any registration and card charges shown. Save the receipt.
  6. Wait for RTO approval. The authority verifies eligibility; the BH mark is generated randomly once approved.
  7. Get the HSRP fitted and the RC issued. The RC is also available digitally; keep that copy accessible when you travel.

Paying the next two-year tax block

BH tax is not a one-time lifetime payment. When a block ends, the next block is paid electronically, in practice through the Pay Your Tax service under Vehicle Related Services on Parivahan, using the registration number and chassis details. You can also pay for a multiple of two years where the portal offers it. After the fourteenth year, tax is annual at half the earlier amount. Set your own reminder: no fixed Kerala late fee for a missed BH block could be verified on an official page in this run, so do not rely on a figure quoted elsewhere.

Selling a BH vehicle

Eligibility belongs to the owner, not the vehicle alone. If the buyer is not eligible for BH, the vehicle has to move to a normal state registration in the buyers name, with the tax position worked out by the RTO then. Do not promise a buyer that the BH mark will simply continue. The general transfer paperwork is in our RC transfer Kerala fees and time guide; settle the BH question with the RTO before money changes hands.

FAQs

Can anyone in Kerala get a BH number plate?

No. It is voluntary but restricted to defence personnel, government and PSU employees, and private sector employees whose employer has offices in four or more states or union territories, and only for non-transport vehicles.

Do I need to re-register if I move from Kerala to another state on a BH plate?

No new registration mark is needed on relocation; that is the core benefit notified in 2021. Keep tax blocks paid and insurance, PUC and RC valid in every state.

Is BH road tax in Kerala 8, 10 or 12 percent?

Those are the central rates in Rule 51B(2). The Kerala High Court held in January 2025 that they are not enforceable and that BH vehicles in Kerala are taxed under the Kerala Motor Vehicle Taxation Act, 1976. Go by your Vahan payment screen, not the central table.

Can I convert my existing KL vehicle to BH?

The series was launched for new vehicles, and this run could not verify an official Kerala conversion procedure for existing KL vehicles on parivahan.gov.in or mvd.kerala.gov.in. Ask your RTO, with eligibility proof, before relying on any conversion claim by an agent or dealer.

Can I choose a favourite BH number?

No. The BH registration mark is generated randomly through the online portal, as four randomised digits followed by two randomised letters after the year and BH code.
