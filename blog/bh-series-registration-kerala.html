BH series registration in Kerala

Last updated: October 2026

The direct answer: BH (Bharat) series registration is a voluntary, all-India registration mark for non-transport (private) vehicles only, available in Kerala if you fall in an eligible employment category: defence personnel, Central or State government employees, Central or State public sector undertaking employees, or a private sector employee whose employer has offices in four or more states or union territories. A BH vehicle does not need a new registration mark when you move to another state, and its motor vehicle tax is paid in blocks of two years instead of as one lifetime payment. The Kerala point most guides miss is tax: after a Kerala High Court ruling in January 2025, BH vehicles registered in Kerala are taxed under the Kerala Motor Vehicle Taxation Act, 1976, not at the flat central rates quoted on most national pages, so the tax amount shown on the Vahan payment screen for your vehicle is the figure to go by.

What the BH number looks like

The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways introduced the BH series by notification dated 26 August 2021 (G.S.R. 594(E), the Central Motor Vehicles (Twentieth Amendment) Rules, 2021). The mark is generated randomly through the online portal, so you cannot choose the number.

Part of the markMeaning
YY (first two digits)Year of first registration, for example 26 for 2026
BHCode for the Bharat series
#### (four digits)Randomised number from 0000 to 9999
XX (two letters)Randomised alphabets from AA to ZZ

A plate reading 26 BH 1234 AB therefore tells you the vehicle was first registered in 2026 under the Bharat series. It carries no Kerala district code, unlike a normal KL plate, which is the whole point for owners who move between states for work. The plate itself is a high security registration plate; our HSRP number plate Kerala price guide covers fitment in Kerala.

Who is eligible in Kerala

CategoryProof of eligibility
Defence personnelOfficial service identity card
Central or State government employeesOfficial identity card
Central or State PSU employeesOfficial identity card or employer certificate
Private sector employeesForm 60 working certificate from the employer, confirming the company has offices in four or more states or union territories, plus the employment identity card

If none of these categories fits you, you are not eligible, however often you travel. BH is also not available for transport vehicles such as taxis, goods vehicles or buses. Self-employed applicants, and employees of companies that operate only in Kerala or in fewer than four states, stay on the normal KL series. Eligibility is checked by the registering authority from the documents above, and an application with weak employment proof is the common reason a BH request is not approved.

Road tax for BH vehicles in Kerala: read this before quoting any rate

Lead with the official Kerala position, because national pages quote a central table that does not set your Kerala bill. Rule 51B of the Central Motor Vehicle Rules sets the principles for BH tax: tax is levied for two years or in a multiple of two, and after the fourteenth year it is levied annually at half of the amount charged earlier for that vehicle. Rule 51B(2) also printed central rates of 8 percent of invoice price below Rs.10 lakh, 10 percent from Rs.10 to 20 lakh and 12 percent above Rs.20 lakh, with 2 percent extra for diesel vehicles and 2 percent less for electric vehicles.

In January 2025 the Kerala High Court, in Harish Kumar KP v Union of India (2025 LiveLaw (Ker) 13), held that prescribing the rate of tax is within the states domain, that the central rates in Rule 51B(2) are not enforceable, and that the petitioners vehicles should be registered under the BH series with tax charged under the Kerala Motor Vehicle Taxation Act, 1976, following the principles in Rule 51B(1) and (3). The practical result for a Kerala applicant is simple: the two-year block pattern applies, but the rate is the Kerala Act rate, and no separate Kerala BH rate table could be verified on parivahan.gov.in or mvd.kerala.gov.in in this run (mvd.kerala.gov.in did not respond at research time). Treat the tax total shown on the Vahan payment screen for your own vehicle as final, and keep the receipt. Any dealer quote should be checked against that screen before you pay; a gap between a quote and the official screen total is normally a service charge or an add-on, so ask for an itemised breakup.

No separate BH-specific registration fee could be verified on an official fee page in this run either. Registration, smart card and plate charges, if they apply to your application, appear in the same Vahan fee panel, and that panel is the final figure.

Documents to keep ready

  • Form 20, the application for registration of a motor vehicle (filed on Vahan, usually through the dealer for a new vehicle)
  • Proof of eligibility: official identity card for government, PSU and defence applicants; Form 60 working certificate and employment identity card for private sector applicants
  • Identity and address proof, such as Aadhaar, passport or voter ID
  • Vehicle invoice or sale certificate from the dealer
  • Valid motor insurance certificate
  • Pollution Under Control certificate, where the portal asks for it
  • PAN or Form 60 details and a passport-size photograph, if prompted during the application

Step by step: apply for BH registration in Kerala

  1. Confirm eligibility first. Match yourself to the table above and get the identity card or Form 60 certificate in hand before the vehicle application starts; eligibility is verified from these papers, not from a declaration alone.
  2. Start the new vehicle registration on Vahan. For a new vehicle this is normally done through the dealer, who files Form 20 on vahan.parivahan.gov.in for the Kerala RTO where the vehicle is being registered.
  3. Select the BH series as the registration type in the application, instead of the normal state series, and enter the vehicle, invoice and owner details exactly as they appear on the documents.
  4. Upload the eligibility and vehicle documents listed above, in the format the portal specifies.
  5. Pay the tax and fees shown on the Vahan screen. This is the first two-year tax block under the Kerala Act, as explained above, plus any registration and card charges the panel shows. Save the payment receipt.
  6. Wait for RTO approval. The registering authority verifies eligibility and the vehicle record; the BH mark is generated randomly by the system once the application is approved.
  7. Get the HSRP fitted and the RC issued. The registration certificate is issued for the BH mark and is also available digitally; keep the digital copy accessible when you travel to other states.

Paying the next two-year tax block

BH tax is not a one-time lifetime payment. When a two-year block ends, the next block is paid electronically, in practice through the Pay Your Tax service under Vehicle Related Services on Parivahan, using the registration number and chassis details. You can also pay for a multiple of two years where the portal offers it. After the fourteenth year, tax is annual and at half the amount charged earlier, on the Rule 51B principle the Kerala High Court left in place. Set your own reminder for each block: driving on an unpaid tax block risks penalties under the tax law that applies to the vehicle, and no fixed Kerala late fee for a missed BH block could be verified on an official page in this run, so do not rely on a figure quoted elsewhere.

Selling a BH vehicle

Eligibility belongs to the owner, not to the vehicle alone. If you sell a BH vehicle to a buyer who is also eligible for BH, the transfer can be discussed with the RTO on that basis; if the buyer is not eligible, the vehicle has to move to a normal state registration in the buyers name, with the tax position worked out by the RTO at that point. Do not promise a buyer that the BH mark will simply continue. The general transfer paperwork is in our RC transfer Kerala fees and time guide, and the BH eligibility question should be settled with the RTO before money changes hands.

FAQs

Can anyone in Kerala get a BH number plate?

No. It is voluntary but restricted to defence personnel, government and PSU employees, and private sector employees whose employer has offices in four or more states or union territories, and only for non-transport vehicles.

Do I need to re-register if I move from Kerala to another state on a BH plate?

No new registration mark is needed on relocation; that is the core benefit notified in 2021. Keep your tax blocks paid and your insurance, PUC and RC valid, as those obligations continue in every state.

Is BH road tax in Kerala 8, 10 or 12 percent?

Those are the central rates printed in Rule 51B(2). The Kerala High Court held in January 2025 that those central rates are not enforceable and that BH vehicles in Kerala are taxed under the Kerala Motor Vehicle Taxation Act, 1976. Go by the tax shown on your Vahan payment screen, not by the central table.

Can I convert my existing KL vehicle to BH?

The series was launched for new vehicles, and this run could not verify a current, official Kerala conversion procedure for existing KL vehicles on parivahan.gov.in or mvd.kerala.gov.in. Ask the RTO where the vehicle is registered, with your eligibility proof, before relying on any conversion claim made by an agent or dealer.

Can I choose a favourite BH number?

No. The BH registration mark is generated randomly through the online portal, as four randomised digits followed by two randomised letters after the year and BH code.
