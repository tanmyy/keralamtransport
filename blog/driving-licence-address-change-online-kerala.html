Driving licence address change online in Kerala

Last updated: October 2026

Yes, you can apply for a driving licence address change online in Kerala on sarathi.parivahan.gov.in, and the official fee is Rs.200. Parivahan's Licensing Related Fees and Charges page, checked in October 2026, lists Rs.200 for "any application for change in address or any other particulars recorded in the driving licence". You enter your DL number and date of birth, choose Change of Address in DL, type the new address, upload proof of the new address, and pay online. The RTO that covers your new address then scrutinises the application, and the updated licence is issued after approval. Keep the application number from the acknowledgement: it is how you track the file until the updated card reaches you.

That Rs.200 is the official service fee on Parivahan's fee page. The payment screen on Sarathi is the final word on your total: if the portal adds a card or postal charge for your application, it will show there before you pay, and you should pay only the total shown on the official portal. I could not verify a separate, Kerala-specific card charge for this service on an official page, so do not treat totals quoted on private agent or help sites as the Kerala figure. If you apply through an Akshaya centre or another help desk, any amount above the portal total is a service charge for their help (ask for a receipt), not a higher official fee.

Documents you need

  • Your existing driving licence: you need the DL number and date of birth to open the application, and a scanned copy to upload if the portal asks for it.
  • Proof of the new address: one document showing the address exactly as you want it printed. Accepted examples used across this site's Parivahan checklists are a passport, voter ID, Aadhaar, an electricity or telephone bill from the last 3 months, a bank passbook, or a registered rent agreement.
  • Photograph and signature: upload scans in the format the portal specifies if that step appears in your application.

Two practical points cause most rejections. First, spell the new address the same way on the form and on the proof: a different house name, ward or PIN code between the two is enough for scrutiny to query the file. Second, if your only proof is a rent agreement or a utility bill in a family member's or landlord's name, keep an additional supporting document ready and follow any direction the RTO gives at scrutiny, rather than uploading a document that shows a different address.

How to change your DL address online, step by step

  1. Open parivahan.gov.in and choose Online Services, then Driving Licence Related Services.
  2. Select Kerala as the state. If your licence was issued in another state, read the interstate section below before you start.
  3. Choose the DL services option (labelled Services on DL or Apply Online in the Sarathi menu) and enter your driving licence number and date of birth.
  4. Confirm that the licence details shown are yours, and give your mobile number and other contact details the form asks for.
  5. Select the RTO that covers your new address, then choose Change of Address in DL from the list of services.
  6. Enter the new address carefully. Sarathi records a present address and a permanent address: change the one that has actually changed, or both if you have moved fully.
  7. Upload the proof of the new address, and your photograph and signature if the portal asks for them.
  8. Pay the fee online (official fee Rs.200, plus anything the payment screen itself adds) and save the acknowledgement and application number.
  9. Track the file under Application Status on Sarathi using the application number and your date of birth.
  10. After RTO scrutiny and approval, the updated licence is printed and sent to the address on the licence. Once the record is updated, the new address also shows on the digital licence in DigiLocker and mParivahan.

Do you have to visit the RTO?

Many address-change applications are processed from the online file alone, with the RTO scrutinising the uploaded proof. That is not a guarantee for every file. If the proof is unclear, the address does not match, or your licence record is an old one that is not fully digitised, the RTO can call you in with the originals. Watch the application status instead of assuming silence means approval: a file held at scrutiny usually needs a document fix or a visit, and it will sit there until you act. Carry the original licence, the original address proof and the acknowledgement if you do go.

How long does it take?

Parivahan does not publish a fixed Kerala timeline for a DL address change, and this post will not invent one. The honest answer is that the time depends on scrutiny at your RTO: a clean file with a matching address proof moves as soon as that RTO processes its queue, while a queried file takes as long as the query takes to resolve. Use the application number to check the status on Sarathi, and treat any exact number of days quoted on a private site as that writer's experience, not an official promise. Do not book travel or an ID check that depends on the new card until the status shows approval and printing.

Moving into Kerala from another state

If your licence was issued outside Kerala and you have now moved here permanently, the application is still started on Sarathi, but expect an extra step: secondary guides to the process describe entering No Objection Certificate or clearance details from the RTO that issued the licence, including the NOC number and date, along with proof of the new Kerala address. Because the exact requirement depends on how your issuing RTO recorded the licence, confirm the NOC position for your file on Sarathi or with the destination RTO before paying, and do not rely on a guide written for another state. Your vehicle is a separate record: changing the DL address does not change the address on your RC, and a vehicle brought into Kerala has its own re-registration and tax questions, covered in our guide to NOC for car transfer from Kerala to another state for the outward direction.

DL address change is not an RC address change

These are two different records held by two different Parivahan systems: Sarathi for licences and Vahan for vehicles. Approving a new address on your driving licence does not update your registration certificate, your insurance record or your challan contact details. If you have moved house, work through the list: DL address on Sarathi, RC address on Vahan, and the address held by your insurer. FAQ

What is the official fee for a driving licence address change in Kerala?

Rs.200. Parivahan's Licensing Related Fees and Charges page lists Rs.200 for any application for change in address or any other particulars recorded in the driving licence. Pay only the total shown on the official Sarathi payment screen for your application.

Which address proof is safest to upload?

A document in your own name that shows the new address exactly as entered, such as Aadhaar, a passport or a voter ID, or a recent electricity bill. A mismatch between the form and the proof is the most common reason for a scrutiny query.

Does an address change give me a fresh licence validity?

No. The updated licence keeps the validity of the existing licence. An address change only corrects the recorded address; renewal is a separate service with its own fee.

Will I get a new card?

The approved change is recorded on your licence and a card carrying the new address is issued through the normal printing and dispatch route for your RTO. The digital licence in DigiLocker and mParivahan reflects the updated record once it is approved.

Can I change the address while renewing the licence?

Yes, an address change can be taken up along with a renewal application on Sarathi, and each service carries its own fee. If renewal is due soon anyway, combining the two avoids a second card being printed with the old address.

What if my licence is not found on Sarathi?

Older licences are not always fully digitised. If your DL number and date of birth return no record, the licence details first need to be entered in the system through your RTO before an online address change can be filed. Visit the issuing RTO with the original licence.
