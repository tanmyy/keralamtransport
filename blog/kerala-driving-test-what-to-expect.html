Kerala driving test: what to expect and how to prepare

Applicants describe a test with three parts: the 8, the H, and a road test. The rules changed in 2024 and people say the new course feels different from the old H-only test. Kerala's driving test is India's toughest by reputation. Be honest about your practice hours.

Practise on the real ground if you can

One applicant who failed the H test three times said the test ground differed from their practice ground, then passed later. Others say the reference rods still exist on the ground, so learn to judge distance from the driver's seat instead of relying on one marker.

Slots fill fast

Applicants say booking opens in the morning, around 9 or 10 a.m., and slots can be full within minutes. Some report waits of several months. Keep your learner's licence valid while you wait, and budget for a renewal. Fees for that are on our learner's licence page.

Driving school or self-service?

Many applicants get the learner's licence themselves online and use a driving school for practice. Others use a school because it helps with slot booking. A school's price can include lessons, test-day support and add-ons, so ask for an itemised quote.
