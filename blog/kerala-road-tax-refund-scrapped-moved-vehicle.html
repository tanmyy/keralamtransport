Road tax refund in Kerala when a vehicle is scrapped or moved to another state

Last updated: October 2026

Yes, you can claim a road tax refund in Kerala when your vehicle leaves the state or is taken off the road for good, but only on the conditions the law sets. Section 6(2) of the Kerala Motor Vehicles Taxation Act, 1976 says a registered owner who has paid tax for a year or more is entitled to a refund at prescribed rates when the registration is cancelled, or when the vehicle is removed to a place outside Kerala on account of transfer of ownership or change of address. The refund is claimed at the RTO where the vehicle is registered in Kerala, after the move or the scrapping is complete, and it is paid to your bank account after the RTO verifies your papers. Green tax is not refundable.

The key point for most private car owners is the one-time tax: Kerala collects it for 15 years at registration, so when the car leaves Kerala in year 6, the tax for the years after the move is the money you are claiming back. The same logic applies to a scrapped vehicle: the tax for the period after cancellation is what comes back, not the whole amount.

How the refund amount is worked out

The Act does not print a refund table in Section 6 itself. It says the refund is paid at rates prescribed by the government, and the RTO computes it from the tax you paid, the tax period it covered, and the date the vehicle left Kerala or the registration was cancelled. Treat the figure on the RTO's own calculation as final.

Documents to keep ready

For a vehicle moved out of Kerala, you will need: the original Kerala tax payment receipt, the old Kerala RC, the NOC issued by the Kerala RTO, the new registration certificate from the other state, the new state's tax payment receipt, your ID proof, and your bank account details for the refund credit. For a scrapped vehicle, you will need: the original Kerala tax payment receipt, the RC, the scrapping certificate or certificate of deposit from the registered scrapping facility, and proof that the registration has been cancelled. In both cases, keep copies of everything you submit, and keep the bank account in the registered owner's name.

Steps to claim the refund

  1. Finish the trigger event first. For a move, complete re-registration in the new state and pay its tax; for scrapping, complete the scrapping and get the registration cancelled. A refund claim made before this is complete has nothing to attach to.
  2. Go to the RTO or Joint RTO where the vehicle is registered in Kerala, and file the refund application under Section 6 of the Kerala Motor Vehicles Taxation Act, 1976, read with Rule 15 of the Kerala Motor Vehicles Taxation Rules. State the reason clearly: removal outside Kerala, or cancellation of registration.
  3. Attach the documents listed above, with the Kerala tax receipt and the proof of the move or the scrapping on top of the file.
  4. The RTO verifies the file, which for an interstate move includes checking the re-registration details with the new state. Expect this to take time: guides consistently describe the process as taking weeks to months, and no fixed Kerala processing timeline could be verified on an official page.
  5. Once approved, the refund is credited to the registered owner's bank account. Keep the approval and the credit advice with the vehicle's old papers.

Three traps that cost people their refund

First, do not claim before the move or the scrapping is finished. The claim rests on the cancellation or the removal, and without that proof the file cannot be verified. Second, do not claim a non-use refund for a period when the vehicle was actually used. Section 5 of the Act allows a non-use claim only on a prior written intimation to the RTO, and if verification finds the vehicle was used during the declared period, the exemption is lost and additional tax can follow; guides describe the exposure as up to twice the tax value, which is a reported figure, not a verified schedule in this post. Third, do not expect the green tax back, and do not expect a refund when the buyer is inside Kerala: neither case falls under Section 6(2).

FAQs

Can I claim a refund if I move out of Kerala but keep the Kerala registration?

No. The refund under Section 6(2) follows removal outside Kerala on transfer of ownership or change of address, which in practice means the vehicle is re-registered in the new state. A Kerala-registered vehicle still on Kerala plates has not been removed in the sense the Act requires.

Do I get the full tax back if I scrap a 3-year-old car?

No. Only the tax for the period after cancellation is refundable, at the prescribed rates. The tax for the years the vehicle was on the road in Kerala stays paid.

Is there an online refund application on Parivahan?

No online Kerala tax refund service could be verified at research time. The refund is an RTO counter process at the registering office, and the RTO's calculation is final.

What if my vehicle was financed?

Get the financier's consent and NOC in order before the move or the scrapping, as for any transfer or cancellation. A pending loan does not remove the refund right, but an incomplete NOC file stalls verification.
