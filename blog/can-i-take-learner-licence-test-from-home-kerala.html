Can I take the learner's licence test from home in Kerala?

Last updated: September 2026

No. As of September 2026, Kerala does not offer the learner's licence test from home. The test is taken in person, on computers at your RTO or test centre. Almost everything else in the process, the application, document upload, fee payment, tutorial videos and mock tests, can be done from home, but the actual test needs you physically present. I could not find any official Kerala MVD notice offering a home-based test, and the state's toughened test format (30 questions plus a hazard-perception simulation) is designed to run on centre hardware.

What you CAN do from home

There is real confusion here because so much of the process has genuinely moved online. From home, on sarathi.parivahan.gov.in, you can:

  • Fill Form 2 and apply for the learner's licence, with Aadhaar OTP verification
  • Upload your age proof, address proof, photograph and signature
  • Pay the official fee online: Rs.150 for the licence issue plus Rs.50 for the test, per vehicle class
  • Book your test slot at your KL-coded RTO
  • Prepare with tutorial videos and mock tests. The Kerala MVD's official MVD LEADS app offers syllabi, practice sets and mock tests, and completing a mock test there earns a road-safety certificate that exempts you from classroom-based training

So the trip to the RTO is really just for the test itself. Our full walkthrough of the online part is in how to apply for a learner's licence online in Kerala.

Why the test itself stays in person in Kerala

Kerala overhauled its licence testing from 1 October 2025, becoming the first Indian state to do so. The reformed learner's test has:

  • 30 questions, up from 20, on traffic rules, signs and safety
  • 18 correct answers needed to pass (a 60% pass mark); the Transport Minister has proposed raising this to 25
  • 30 seconds per question, and a skip option that lets you leave questions unanswered without penalty
  • Negative marking: 0.25 points deducted for every wrong answer
  • Hazard perception testing: 10 simulation scenarios where you react to on-screen obstacles in real time, needing 30 out of 50 marks to pass

The hazard-perception module in particular is built for supervised centre setups with calibrated screens and response measurement, which is one reason a webcam-proctored home version has not appeared in Kerala. Test vehicles and tracks aside, the learner's test is still a controlled-room exercise: your slot, your seat, the RTO's computer.

STALL: the home test some other states use

The national Sarathi system does have a home-based option called STALL (Sarathi Test for All Learner's Licences): an AI-proctored, Aadhaar-authenticated online test you can take from a laptop or phone with a webcam. Several states have switched it on, and news reports from September 2026 describe applicants completing the whole learner's licence flow from home in those states.

But STALL is a state-by-state choice, and I could not verify that Kerala MVD has enabled it. The Kerala MVD's own site did not load during my research, and no Kerala-specific notice of a home test surfaced in news coverage. Given the October 2025 reforms and the centre-based hazard perception module, the practical position is: plan to take the test at your RTO. If Kerala ever switches on a home mode, it will be announced on mvd.kerala.gov.in and on the Sarathi portal itself, not on a third-party blog.

Watch out for fake "home test" offers

This is the part of this topic that matters most. Any person or website promising to arrange a learner's licence from home in Kerala, or charging you extra for one, is offering something Kerala MVD does not currently provide. The real process has one fee point and one payment channel:

Fee itemOfficial amount

Pay only on parivahan.gov.in or sarathi.parivahan.gov.in, and save the receipt. If you use a driving school or an Akshaya centre to file the application, treat anything they charge above this table as their service charge and ask for an itemised quote. A test that never happens at a real centre cannot produce a real licence, no matter who collects the money.

Steps: apply from home, test at the RTO

  1. Apply online. On sarathi.parivahan.gov.in, select Kerala, choose Apply for Learner Licence, complete the Aadhaar OTP verification, and fill Form 2 with your details and vehicle class. Step-by-step: how to apply for a learner's licence online in Kerala.
  2. Upload documents and pay. Age proof, address proof, photograph, signature, Form 1 fitness self-declaration (Form 1A medical certificate if you are above 40 or applying for a transport class). Pay Rs.150 + Rs.50 per class online.
  3. Prepare. Use the MVD LEADS app mock tests and our Kerala learner's licence question bank with answers. Completing a mock test on the app earns the road-safety certificate that exempts you from classroom training.
  4. Book your slot. Choose the RTO under your address and pick a date. Slots at busy RTOs fill fast, and reported waits have stretched to months at some centres, so book as soon as you are ready.
  5. Take the test at the RTO. Bring the slot confirmation, your application number and originals of the documents. You get 30 seconds per question, can skip without penalty, and need at least 18 right out of 30 (plus the hazard perception pass).
  6. Download the licence. On passing, the learner's licence is issued and downloadable from the portal. It is valid for six months; you can apply for the permanent licence 30 days later. Read what the Kerala driving test involves before that stage.

FAQs

Can I take the Kerala learner's licence test from home in September 2026?

No. Kerala has not enabled a home-based test that I could verify. The computer test is taken at the RTO or test centre.

What is STALL, and why do other websites say the test is from home?

STALL is the national Sarathi home-test system (AI-proctored, Aadhaar-verified) that some states have adopted. Articles about those states describe a from-home process, but it does not apply to Kerala unless Kerala MVD switches it on.

What could not be verified while writing this?

Two things, stated plainly: the Kerala MVD website did not load during research, so I could not check for any new notice on home testing; and I could not confirm Kerala's exact current slot-booking flow beyond what the Sarathi portal shows. The portal itself is the final word.

Can I take the test in Malayalam?

Yes. The test offers Malayalam, English and Hindi, chosen at the start of the test.

What happens if I fail?

You can reappear after 7 days by paying only the Rs.50 test fee again. The skip option means guessing is worse than skipping: a wrong answer costs 0.25, a skip costs nothing.

Can I apply in another state with a home test instead?

The learner's licence is tied to the state and RTO you apply through, and your address proof should match. Applying elsewhere to dodge a test format can complicate the later driving test and address records, so it is not a shortcut worth taking.
