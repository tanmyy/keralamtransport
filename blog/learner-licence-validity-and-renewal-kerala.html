Learner licence validity and renewal in Kerala

Last updated: October 2026

A learner licence in Kerala is valid for six months from the date of issue, and it works the same way across India. It is not renewed in the way a driving licence is renewed. The Licensing Related Fees page on parivahan.gov.in, checked for this post in October 2026, lists no separate learner licence renewal fee at all: it lists only the issue fee of Rs.150 per class of vehicle and a learner licence test fee of Rs.50. If your learner licence expires before you pass the driving test, you stop learning legally, and you get a fresh period by using the Expired Learner Licence Issue Again service on Sarathi Parivahan, paying the issue fee again for a new six-month licence.

The validity timeline, in one table

StageRule
Learner licence issuedValid for 6 months from the issue date printed on the licence (Form 3)
Earliest driving licence applicationAfter holding the learner licence for at least 30 days
Driving testMust be taken while the learner licence is still valid
Learner licence expiredNo longer valid for learning or for the driving test; get it issued again or apply fresh

Work backwards from the expiry date, not forwards from the issue date. A safer plan is to book the driving test in the second or third month, leaving time for a retest if you fail. Our guide to when to apply for a permanent driving licence after a learner permit in Kerala covers that 30-day wait and the test window in more detail.

What happens when a learner licence expires

  • You cannot practise: driving or riding as a learner on an expired learner licence is the same, in legal terms, as learning without a licence. The supervision and L-board rules only protect you while the learner licence itself is valid.
  • You cannot sit the driving test on it: the test is a test of competence taken on the strength of a valid learner licence for that class. An expired licence will not carry you through the test-day checks.
  • Your practice time does not transfer as validity: months spent learning on the old licence do not extend the new one. A licence issued again starts a fresh six-month period from its own issue date.
  • No grace period could be verified: no learner licence grace period after expiry could be verified on the Parivahan pages checked for this post. Treat the expiry date printed on your licence as the last valid day, and follow the dates Sarathi shows for your own record.

Is there a renewal, or is it a fresh issue?

This is where most confusion comes from, because three different words are used for the same recovery. Applicants and help pages often say renewal, Sarathi says issue again, and the fee table simply says issue. In substance, they point to the same outcome: the old licence is not extended by a renewal payment the way a driving licence is. Instead, a new learner licence is issued, linked to your expired learner licence number, with its own new six-month validity. Follow the Kerala flow on Sarathi for a Kerala licence.

If Sarathi does not accept your expired licence number, for example because the record is very old or the details do not match, the fallback is a completely fresh learner licence application, with the learner test again. The steps for a first application are in how to apply for learner licence online in Kerala, and the learner licence service page summarises the service.

Official fees: what issuing again costs

Lead with the official fee. This table is from the Licensing Related Fees page on parivahan.gov.in, checked in October 2026. As noted above, that page lists no separate renewal or reissue fee for a learner licence.

Fee itemOfficial amount
Issue of learner licence, per class of vehicleRs.150
Learner licence test fee, or repeat test feeRs.50

For one class, that is Rs.200 at the learner stage. For two classes taken together, such as a motorcycle class and a car, the issue fee is charged per class, which is why Kerala applicants commonly see Rs.350 for two classes: Rs.150 twice, plus the Rs.50 test fee once. Treat the total Sarathi shows on your payment screen as final, because no separate Kerala-specific reissue charge could be verified on an official Kerala page for this post, and mvd.kerala.gov.in was unreachable at research time. If a driving school or agent helps you file, anything above the official components is their service charge: ask for an itemised quote before you pay.

Step by step: expired learner licence issue again on Sarathi

  1. Open sarathi.parivahan.gov.in, select Kerala as the state, and open the Learner Licence menu.
  2. Select Expired Learner Licence Issue Again. This is the service Sarathi provides for an expired learner licence, instead of a driving licence style renewal.
  3. Enter your expired learner licence number and date of birth, and complete the OTP verification on the mobile number linked to the record.
  4. Check the details pulled up: name, class or classes of vehicle, and the RTO. If a class is missing or a detail is wrong, stop and correct it with the RTO rather than paying on a wrong application.
  5. Upload any documents Sarathi asks for on that screen. Keep your expired learner licence, age proof and address proof ready in case they are requested again.
  6. Pay the fees shown: expect the Rs.150 issue fee per class and the Rs.50 test fee from the official table above. Save the application number and the payment receipt.
  7. Take the learner licence test again if Sarathi directs you to: whether a repeat test is required on an issue-again application is shown in that flow for your record, and no Kerala-specific rule on this point could be verified on an official page for this post. If a test is set, prepare with Kerala learner licence test questions with answers.
  8. Download and print the new learner licence (Form 3) when it is generated, and note the new issue and expiry dates. The new six-month period runs from this issue date.
  9. After at least 30 days on the new licence, apply for the driving licence and book the test while the new licence is still valid, using how to book a driving licence test slot in Kerala.

How to avoid needing a second learner licence

  • Book early: apply for the driving licence as soon as the 30-day wait ends, instead of waiting until month five or six.
  • Leave a retest buffer: a failed test needs a new slot and a repeat test fee. Failing in the last week of validity often means the learner licence expires before the retest.
  • Watch the printed expiry date: it is on the learner licence itself, not six months from when you started practising or paid the fee.
  • Do not practise after expiry while you wait: wait for the new learner licence to be generated before resuming supervised practice.

FAQs

How long is a learner licence valid in Kerala?

Six months from the date of issue. The licence is valid across India during that period, under the learner conditions: supervision by a permanent licence holder and an L sign displayed on the vehicle.

Can I renew my learner licence before it expires?

There is no advance renewal that extends a live learner licence. The expected route is to finish the driving test within the six months. The issue-again service is for a licence that has already expired.

What is the fee for learner licence renewal in Kerala?

Parivahan lists no separate renewal fee. Issuing the licence again costs the issue fee from the official table: Rs.150 per class, plus the Rs.50 learner licence test fee. The total on your Sarathi payment screen is final.

Is there a grace period after my learner licence expires?

No learner licence grace period could be verified on the official pages checked for this post. Treat the printed expiry date as the last day you can learn or test on that licence.

Does the 30-day wait start again on a licence issued again?

Plan on it. The driving licence application is tied to a valid learner licence, and the safe reading is that the 30 days run from the new licence's issue date. Follow the earliest test date Sarathi allows for your application rather than assuming the first licence's dates carry over.

Can I apply for a permanent driving licence with an expired learner licence?

No. Get the learner licence issued again first, hold it for the required period, and then apply and test while it is valid.
