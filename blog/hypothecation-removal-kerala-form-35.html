Hypothecation removal in Kerala: Form 35, NOC and the RTO steps

Last updated: October 2026

You have paid off your vehicle loan, but the bank's name is still printed on your Registration Certificate. That entry is the hypothecation, and it does not come off by itself. In Kerala, removing it means collecting the lender's No Objection Certificate (NOC) and Form 35, the prescribed termination form under Rule 61 of the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, signed by both you and the lender, then applying on the Kerala MVD portal under Cancel Hypothecation and submitting the papers at your RTO. The official fee is the Rule 81 amount, shown on the payment screen; confirm it there before paying. Below: the document checklist, the fee picture, the numbered steps, the 90-day NOC window, and what to do after the RC comes back clean.

Why the lender's name stays on your RC

When a vehicle is bought on loan, the lender's interest is entered on the RC under Section 51 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988. Repaying the loan ends the debt but not the record. The entry is removed only on an application in Form 35, duly signed by the registered owner and the financier, accompanied by the RC and the fee prescribed under Rule 81, per Parivahan's HP termination page.

An uncleared hypothecation blocks resale: the RTO cannot record a transfer of ownership without the financier's written consent, and insurers can scrutinise claims when the RC still shows a lender interest.

Hypothecation removal fees in Kerala

Parivahan's HP termination page names the fee as the one specified in Rule 81 of the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989, without publishing the rupee amount. The portal shows the exact figure on the payment screen before you pay. What can be said without guessing:

ComponentWhat is known
RTO filing feeThe Rule 81 amount, shown on the payment screen. Applicant reports put the counter fee around Rs.100 in several states; a published Kerala applicant walkthrough reports Rs.75 paid on the Kerala MVD portal (older report, may have changed).
New RC / smart card and postal chargesAdditional lines on the receipt for printing and dispatch of the updated RC.
Assistance charges (optional)Applicant-reported service charges for assisted filing, commonly Rs.500 to Rs.2,000, on top of the official fee.

The NOC itself is typically issued free by the lender, though some lenders charge a nominal re-issue fee if you misplace the originals or the 90-day validity lapses. Never treat a figure on a blog as the fee: read the amount on the payment screen and cross-check it against the Rule 81 schedule before paying.

Step-by-step: how to remove hypothecation in Kerala

  1. Confirm the loan is fully closed. Wait for the final EMI to clear, get the lender's zero-dues confirmation, and keep the closure letter.
  2. Request the NOC and Form 35 in writing. Cite your loan account and registration numbers. Major lenders commonly issue the NOC within 7 to 15 working days; keep a copy of your request.
  3. Verify the documents when they arrive. The registration number on the NOC must match the RC exactly, the NOC must be signed by an authorised officer, and Form 35 must be signed by you and by the lender's authorised signatory. An unsigned or name-mismatched Form 35 is a common reason for rejection.
  4. Apply on the Kerala MVD portal. Go to mvd.kerala.gov.in, open Vehicles, then Apply Online, and choose Cancel Hypothecation. Select your RTO, enter the registration number and chassis number, pay the fee shown on screen, and print the receipt.
  5. Visit your RTO promptly. A published Kerala applicant walkthrough notes the RTO visit should happen within 3 working days of the online payment, so plan to pay just before you go rather than weeks ahead. If your RTO is Ernakulam, our KL-07 RTO profile page shows the kind of office details to check before the visit.
  6. Submit the file at the counter. Hand over the fee receipt, bank NOC, Form 35 copies, original RC, insurance copy, PUC copy, and ID proof. Keep the inward or acknowledgement number; the RTO processes the endorsement and issues the updated RC.
  7. Track and collect. Check application status on the MVD portal under Vehicles application status. Once approved, collect the updated RC or receive it by post, verify on vahan.parivahan.gov.in that the financier column is blank, and check the updated RC on DigiLocker or the mParivahan app.

The 90-day NOC window

A lender's NOC is commonly valid for only 90 days from the date of issue. After that window, the RTO will not act on it, and you must request a fresh one from the lender. So do not ask for the NOC months before you are ready to file; request it when the loan closure is confirmed and you can reach the RTO within weeks. If you are selling, start the termination before listing: a buyer who sees the lender's name on the RC treats the vehicle as encumbered.

What to do after the RC is clean

  • Update your insurer in writing. Share the updated RC so the policy record matches; some insurers ask for a copy of the new RC or an endorsement on the policy.
  • Keep the paperwork permanently. Store the original NOC, the signed Form 35 copies, and the loan closure letter; buyers and RTOs can ask for them later.
  • Then handle the ownership transfer if you are selling. With hypothecation cleared, the transfer proceeds normally. Our ownership transfer page covers the steps, documents, and the 14-day notification rule.

If the lender delays your NOC

Keep the paper trail: your written request, delivery proof, and dates. If the branch does not respond within a reasonable period, escalate to the bank's nodal or grievance officer. If that fails, complaints against banks and covered NBFCs can be filed free under the RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme at cms.rbi.org.in, but you must first complain to the lender and allow 30 days for a reply. For a stuck RTO application, use the Vahan feedback system or CPGRAMS.

FAQ

Does hypothecation come off automatically after the last EMI?

No. The loan account closes, but the RC entry stays until you file Form 35 with the NOC at the RTO.

Can I do this fully online in Kerala without visiting the RTO?

The application and fee go through the Kerala MVD portal, but you must visit the RTO within 3 working days of paying to complete the submission, per applicant reports. Carry the originals.

Is there a deadline for filing after loan closure?

No statutory deadline was found, but the practical limit is the NOC's own validity, commonly 90 days from issue. File promptly so the documents stay valid.

The name on my NOC is spelled differently from my RC. What should I do?

Get the lender to correct it before filing. Name mismatches between the NOC, Form 35, and RC are a common reason RTOs reject the application.

Can I check my application status online?

Yes. Use the application or transaction number from your fee receipt in the Vehicles application status section of mvd.kerala.gov.in, or the application number in the Vahan citizen services portal.

Where is the official fee published?

The official fee is the one prescribed under Rule 81 of the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989, and the exact amount is shown on the payment screen.
