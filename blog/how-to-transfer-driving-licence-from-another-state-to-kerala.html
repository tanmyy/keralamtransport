How to transfer driving licence from another state to Kerala

Last updated: October 2026

The direct answer: you do not need a fresh Kerala licence and you do not take the driving test again. A driving licence issued anywhere in India stays valid across the country, and when you move to Kerala permanently you update the record through the Change of Address service on Sarathi Parivahan, selecting Kerala as the state and the Kerala RTO for your new address. The official fee for any application for a change of address or any other particulars recorded in a driving licence is Rs.200, verified on Parivahan's Licensing Related Fees page. Your vehicle classes and your licence validity stay the same. This guide covers only the licence itself; moving a vehicle to Kerala is a separate RC process.

Do you need an NOC from the old state?

Usually, no, and this is the point most older guides get wrong. The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways advised licensing authorities in 2019 that where a licence record can be verified online in the Sarathi database, an NOC from the original licensing authority is not needed for a renewal or a change of address, and authorities were asked to stop the old manual verification practice and check the particulars online instead. So if Sarathi fetches your licence details when you enter your DL number and date of birth, expect the Kerala application to proceed on that verified record.

There are still cases where an NOC step can appear. Older paper licences and records that were never fully digitised may not be verifiable online, and some application flows still route interstate cases through the Issue of NOC service in the state that issued the licence. If the portal asks for an NOC in your case, apply for Issue of NOC under the old state on Sarathi first, note the NOC number and date, and then file the Kerala change of address application quoting them. No separate fee for the issue of an NOC appears on Parivahan's Licensing Related Fees page, so treat the payment screen for your own application as final and keep its receipt.

What changes and what does not

The transfer updates the address and the licensing authority on your record. Three things do not change: the classes of vehicle you are licensed to drive, the validity dates of the licence, and your driving history. Two things can change: the address printed on the card, which becomes your Kerala address, and, where the issuing authority changes to a Kerala RTO, the licence number itself can be reissued in the Kerala series. If your number changes, update it wherever the old number is recorded, such as insurance and DigiLocker, once the new record shows on Sarathi. Step by step: transfer the licence to Kerala on Sarathi

  1. Check your record first. On Sarathi Parivahan, use the DL details or status lookup with your licence number and date of birth to confirm the record is live in the national database. If it fetches cleanly, the NOC-free route should apply.
  2. Open Driving Licence Related Services and select Kerala. Even though the licence was issued elsewhere, the change of address application is filed in the state you have moved to.
  3. Choose Apply for Change of Address. Enter the existing DL number and date of birth and let the portal fetch your details.
  4. Select the Kerala RTO for your new address and type the Kerala address exactly as it appears on your address proof.
  5. Handle the NOC prompt if one appears. If the flow asks for NOC details and you do not have one, pause, file Issue of NOC under the issuing state, and return with the NOC number and date.
  6. Upload the documents from the table above, in the formats and sizes the portal specifies.
  7. Pay the fee online and save the payment receipt and the application acknowledgement.
  8. Book a slot or visit the RTO only if asked. Some applications are processed fully online; others are called in for verification of originals. Follow what your acknowledgement says.
  9. Track the application with the application number and date of birth, as explained in our guide to checking driving licence status online in Kerala. The updated card is sent to the Kerala address on the application.

Your vehicle is a separate transfer

Transferring your licence does not move your vehicle. A car or bike brought to Kerala permanently needs its own process on Vahan: an NOC from the registering authority, re-registration in Kerala and Kerala road tax, which is different paperwork with different fees. Do not assume the DL application covers the RC, and do not use this guide's fee table for a vehicle transfer.

How long does it take?

No fixed Kerala processing timeline for an interstate licence transfer could be verified on an official page in this run, so this post does not quote one. The honest measure is your own application status on Sarathi: scrutiny, approval and card dispatch each show there, and an application that needs an NOC or an RTO visit will take longer than one verified fully online. Apply well before you need the Kerala address on record for insurance, employment or address proof purposes.

Frequently asked questions

Can I drive in Kerala on a licence from another state?

Yes. An Indian driving licence is valid throughout India. The transfer matters when you have moved permanently, because renewals, duplicates and official correspondence all go to the address and authority on record.

Do I need to take a driving test or a medical test for the transfer?

No new driving test is part of a change of address. A medical certificate is a renewal requirement in the cases where renewal asks for one; it is not a standard part of an address transfer for a licence that is still valid.

What if my licence has already expired?

Deal with the renewal as well as the address change. Sarathi may let you file the services together; each keeps its own official fee, and a renewal filed after the grace period carries the additional delay fee shown in the fee table above.

What if my old licence is a paper licence not showing on Sarathi?

That is the case most likely to need the older route: verification or an NOC from the issuing authority, and possibly a visit to that RTO or the Kerala RTO with the original booklet. Start early, because manual verification is the slow part.

Can I keep two licences, one from each state?

No. You hold one driving licence. The transfer moves that single record to Kerala; it does not create a second licence.
