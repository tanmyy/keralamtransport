Vehicle re-registration after 15 years in Kerala

Last updated: October 2026

A private vehicle in Kerala does not have to be scrapped at 15 years. Its first registration is valid for 15 years from the date of first registration, and after that it can be renewed for 5 years at a time, provided the vehicle is produced for inspection and passes, you file Form 25 on the Vahan portal, and you pay the renewal fee, the fitness test charges and, for a private four-wheeler, green tax of Rs.400 for 5 years. Kerala is not under the Delhi-NCR age ban. The total Vahan shows for your number before payment governs your case.

Who needs it and when to start

This post is about non-transport (private) vehicles: bikes, scooters and cars for personal use. Count the 15 years from the date of first registration on your RC, not from manufacture or from a second-hand purchase. Start before the validity date expires: late applications attract a monthly delay fee, listed below. After the first renewal, the process repeats every 5 years. Transport vehicles follow a different fitness cycle and are not covered here.

Fees: what Parivahan lists

Lead with the official fee, as shown on the Fees and User Charges page of parivahan.gov.in, checked in October 2026. That page lists a general fee for issue or renewal of a certificate of registration, and a separate, higher slab that applies only when the vehicle is older than 20 years from first registration:

MotorcycleRs.300Rs.2,000
Three-wheeler, quadricycle or light motor vehicle, non-transportRs.600Rs.10,000 for a light motor vehicle; Rs.5,000 for a three-wheeler or quadricycle
Imported motorcycleRs.2,500Rs.20,000 for an imported two or three-wheeler
Imported motor vehicleRs.5,000Rs.80,000 for an imported vehicle with four or more wheels

Any other vehicle not listed aboveRs.3,000Rs.12,000

One honest gap: older news reports from 2021 and 2022 quoted a higher 15-year renewal fee of Rs.1,000 for a bike and Rs.5,000 for a car. When I checked the Parivahan fee page in October 2026, it did not list a separate 15 to 20 year slab; it listed the general fees above and the separate slab for vehicles older than 20 years. Do not budget from an old article alone. The fee Vahan calculates for your vehicle at payment is the one you will actually be charged, and it is exclusive of GST where the page says so.

Fitness test and certificate charges

Renewal at 15 years is not a paper-only exercise. The vehicle has to be tested, and Parivahan lists separate charges for conducting the test and for granting or renewing the certificate of fitness:

Green tax at the 15-year renewal

Kerala collects green tax at this renewal. For a private vehicle with four or more wheels over 15 years, the published Kerala rate is Rs.400 for 5 years, paid once per renewal cycle. For two-wheelers and three-wheelers, published reports conflict, so go by the amount Vahan computes for your number. The full category table and payment steps are in our green tax in Kerala guide, and the green tax service page carries the same rate in brief. Any amount a helper or agent quotes above the official fees and taxes in this post is a service charge or add-on: ask for an itemised receipt.

Documents to keep ready

  • Form 25, the application for renewal of the certificate of registration, filled on Vahan or carried as a signed printout if your RTO asks for one
  • Original RC
  • Valid insurance certificate
  • Valid Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate
  • Proof that road tax is paid up to date, and the green tax amount shown on the portal
  • Owner ID and address proof, and PAN card or Form 60 if the portal asks for it
  • A pencil tracing of the chassis number, if your RTO asks for it at inspection
  • Financier NOC, if a loan is still active on the vehicle

Steps: renew on Vahan and complete the inspection

  1. Go to vahan.parivahan.gov.in, open Online Services, then Vehicle Related Services, select Kerala and enter your registration number.
  2. Choose Renewal of Registration from the RC-related services.
  3. Verify the vehicle with the last five characters of the chassis number and the OTP sent to the mobile number registered against the vehicle. If that number is no longer yours, update it first, because the flow depends on it.
  4. Fill Form 25, check the owner and vehicle details on screen, and upload the documents the portal asks for.
  5. Pay what the portal calculates: the renewal fee for your age slab, the fitness test and certificate charges, green tax where it applies, the smart card charge where it applies, and any monthly delay fee if you are late.
  6. Book the inspection slot the portal offers, at your RTO or at the testing station shown for your area.
  7. Take the vehicle for inspection with the original RC, the payment receipts, the printed application and the documents above. The inspector verifies the chassis and engine numbers against the record and checks the condition of the vehicle.
  8. If the vehicle passes, the renewal is approved and a fresh RC is issued with validity for the next 5 years. Track the application with your application number on Vahan until the updated RC is generated.

Preparing for the fitness inspection

Applicants report a practical check, not a cosmetic one. Before your slot, make sure all lights work, including the number plate light, as do the horn and wipers, and that brakes, tyres and number plates are sound and legible. Carry a current PUC. Small scratches are not usually the issue; mismatched chassis numbers, faulty lights and lapsed documents are. If the vehicle does not pass, fix what is listed and present it again, so leave time for a second visit.

If your RC has already expired

Apply anyway, and apply now. The RC delay fee grows by the month, and a daily amount applies after a fitness certificate expires. Do not assume an expired registration is safe to use on the road; complete the renewal first. If the vehicle has been off the road, get it serviced, insured and PUC-tested before booking the inspection.

FAQs

Is a 15-year-old vehicle banned in Kerala?

No. The age-based deregistration rule applies in Delhi-NCR, not in Kerala. In Kerala a private vehicle older than 15 years can be renewed every 5 years if it passes inspection and the fees and green tax are paid.

How long is the renewed registration valid?

Five years. After the first 15 years, each renewal adds 5 more years, and the process, including inspection and green tax for a private four-wheeler, repeats at each renewal.

What is the total cost to re-register a 15-year-old car in Kerala?

Add the items that apply to your vehicle: the renewal fee from the Parivahan table above, the fitness test charge, Rs.200 for grant or renewal of the fitness certificate, Rs.400 green tax for 5 years for a private four-wheeler, and Rs.200 extra if the RC is a smart card, plus any delay fee if you are late. Vahan shows the combined total before you pay; treat that total as final for your vehicle.

Can I do the whole renewal online without visiting the RTO?

No. The application and payment are online on Vahan, but the vehicle itself has to be produced for the fitness inspection. Budget for that one visit, with originals.

Can I sell the vehicle after re-registration?

Yes. A renewed RC is a normal, valid RC, and the vehicle can be transferred like any other. See the ownership transfer steps for what buyer and seller file.

My vehicle is now over 20 years old. Is renewal still possible?

Yes, in Kerala it is, if the vehicle passes inspection, but the renewal fee moves to the higher over-20 slab in the Parivahan table above, which is Rs.2,000 for a motorcycle and Rs.10,000 for a light motor vehicle. Check the slab for your class before you pay.
