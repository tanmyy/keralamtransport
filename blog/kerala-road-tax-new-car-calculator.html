Kerala road tax for new car: calculator, slabs and worked examples

Last updated: October 2026

The direct answer: for a new petrol, diesel or CNG car registered in Kerala you pay a one-time lifetime road tax at the time of registration, calculated as a percentage of the ex-showroom price. The slabs are 10% up to Rs.5 lakh, 13% from Rs.5 to Rs.10 lakh, 15% from Rs.10 to Rs.15 lakh, 17% from Rs.15 to Rs.20 lakh, and 22% above Rs.20 lakh. On top of that comes a Rs.200 one-time road safety cess for a car (light motor vehicle) and a Rs.600 registration fee. Electric cars have separate, lower slabs that were revised in the June 2026 state budget: 3% below Rs.10 lakh, 5% up to Rs.20 lakh, 10% from Rs.20 to Rs.40 lakh, and 15% above Rs.40 lakh. Worked examples and the full tables are below.

The petrol and diesel slab table (2026)

Kerala taxes private cars on their price, not their engine size. There is no separate slab for diesel; petrol, diesel and CNG cars all use the same table. The tax is charged once, at registration, and covers the vehicle's 15-year tax life. These are the slabs current 2026 sources report:

Electric car slabs: revised in the June 2026 budget

Electric cars used to pay 5% to 10% depending on price. As part of the Kerala Budget 2026-27 announced in June 2026, the slabs were redrawn to make mass-market EVs cheaper and luxury EVs dearer:

Note that budget announcements take effect only after the formal notification process, so if you are buying an EV around a budget cycle, confirm with your dealer which slab the registration software is actually applying that week.

Worked examples: do the calculation yourself

  1. Take the ex-showroom price. Use the dealer's ex-showroom figure for your variant, not the on-road price. Discounts you negotiate do not change the tax base; the slab applies to the listed ex-showroom price.
  2. Find your slab in the table above. If your price sits exactly on a boundary (for example, exactly Rs.5,00,000), the Vahan software decides which side you fall on; confirm with the dealer.
  3. Multiply. Road tax = ex-showroom price x slab percentage.
  4. Add the fixed charges: Rs.600 registration fee plus Rs.200 one-time road safety cess for a car. That is the state's side of the bill.

Watch the Rs.20 lakh cliff. Because the slab jumps from 17% to 22% at Rs.20 lakh, two variants of the same model can land very differently. A car at Rs.19,90,000 pays 17%, about Rs.3,38,300. A higher variant at Rs.20,50,000 pays 22%, about Rs.4,51,000. The variant costs Rs.60,000 more, but the tax difference is about Rs.1,13,000. If you are comparing variants that straddle a slab boundary, do this arithmetic before signing.

The Rs.200 road safety cess is levied under section 10 of the Kerala Road Safety Act, 2007, and was raised from Rs.100 to Rs.200 for light motor vehicles in the Kerala Budget 2023. If you want the HSRP booked before delivery, see our HSRP guide for Kerala.

What the tax covers, and what happens later

The lifetime tax covers the vehicle's 15-year registration life. A few consequences buyers often miss:

  • After 15 years, the vehicle attracts a green tax at re-registration (Rs.400 for five years for four-wheelers) and a 50% higher tax rate on renewal. Our green tax guide has the amounts and the Vahan payment steps.
  • Moving to another state does not erase the Kerala tax. You pay the destination state's road tax and can then claim a pro-rata refund of the unused Kerala tax on Form 16, but only the original owner can claim it, and only after re-registration. The full sequence is in our Kerala NOC and road tax refund guide.
  • Selling within Kerala means no fresh tax: the lifetime tax is already paid and transfers with the vehicle. Buyer and seller file Forms 29 and 30; see our RC transfer guide.

Old tables you may still see online

Several pages in search results still show an older slab set (9%, 11%, 13%, 16%, 21%). Those figures appear on pages last updated around 2025 and earlier, and do not match the current set. Kerala has revised its slabs upward over the years, and EV slabs changed again in the June 2026 budget. If a source does not say which year its figures are from, do not trust it for a purchase decision. The Vahan registration software applies the schedule in force on the registration date, which is why this page carries a "last updated" line.

FAQs

How is road tax calculated for a new car in Kerala?

Multiply the ex-showroom price by the slab percentage for your price bracket (10% to 22% for petrol, diesel and CNG cars; 3% to 15% for EVs), then add Rs.600 registration fee and Rs.200 road safety cess. Your dealer usually does this and includes it in the on-road price.

Does a showroom discount reduce the road tax?

No. The tax is calculated on the listed ex-showroom price before discounts, on a flat slab rate. Negotiating Rs.50,000 off does not move your car into a lower slab.

Is road tax the same for petrol and diesel cars in Kerala?

Yes. Unlike some states, Kerala does not penalise diesel cars with a separate slab; petrol, diesel and CNG cars all use the same price-based table.

Is the tax refundable if I move out of Kerala?

Partly. After re-registering the car in the new state, the original owner can claim a pro-rata refund of the unused portion of the Kerala tax from the original RTO. The claim is paperwork-heavy and takes months, so factor that in.

How long is the lifetime tax valid?

Fifteen years, matching the registration validity of a private vehicle. On renewal after 15 years, the tax rate is higher and a green tax applies.

My car is priced exactly at Rs.20 lakh. Which slab applies?

Boundary cases are decided by the registration software's exact comparison logic. Ask your dealer which slab the Vahan portal applies to your specific price, since crossing Rs.20 lakh means 22% instead of 17%.

Does the dealer handle all of this?

Normally, yes. The dealer collects the tax and fee, files the registration with the RTO that covers your address, and hands you the number and RC. The calculation above is still worth knowing, so you can check the dealer's figures against the official slabs.
