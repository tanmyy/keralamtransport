How to pay Kerala road tax online

Last updated: October 2026

You pay Kerala road tax online on the Vahan service inside parivahan.gov.in: open Online Services, choose Vehicle Related Services, select Kerala, choose Pay Your Tax, enter your registration number and the last 5 digits of the chassis number, verify with the OTP sent to the registered mobile number, pick the tax mode, pay, and download the receipt. Most private cars and bikes do not pay this way every year, because their tax was collected once as lifetime tax (15 years) at registration. The Pay Your Tax screen matters most for transport vehicles paying quarterly tax, for a private vehicle at re-registration after 15 years, for vehicles brought into Kerala from another state, and for clearing arrears. Only Vahan can compute your total, from your vehicle record, so the portal total is the figure to pay.

First, work out whether you actually owe tax now

Road tax is a state tax under the Kerala Motor Vehicles Taxation Act, and the period it covers depends on the vehicle class.

VehicleHow tax is normally chargedWhen online payment is used
Private car or bike, first registrationLifetime tax for 15 years, paid once at registration, usually through the dealerNormally nothing separate is due until that period ends
Private vehicle at re-registration after 15 yearsTax for a further 5 years at a time, with the renewalPay the 5-year tax Vahan shows. See our re-registration after 15 years guide; green tax is separate, see our green tax guide
Transport goods vehicleQuarterly tax in the usual case, from the class and weight recordPay each quarter and keep the receipt with the vehicle papers
Transport passenger vehicle (taxi, bus, contract carriage)Quarterly or yearly tax as shown for that permit and seating recordChoose the period Vahan offers and pay before it runs out
Vehicle brought into Kerala from another stateKerala tax becomes due on re-registration or continued use in KeralaPay the amount Vahan computes. New-car rates are in our Kerala road tax calculator

Lifetime does not mean tax is never paid again: it means 15 years were paid in one go at the start. Quarterly does not mean you pick an amount: Vahan works out the tax for the period, and arrears for a late period are added on the same screen.

What you need before you start

  • Registration number exactly as on the RC.
  • Last 5 digits of the chassis number, from the RC or the vehicle itself.
  • The mobile number registered against the vehicle, switched on for the OTP. If that number has changed, update the mobile record first through Vahan services or your RTO.
  • Insurance policy details, because the flow can ask you to confirm or update insurance before the fee panel.
  • A payment method: net banking, debit or credit card, or UPI, as the gateway offers.

No documents are uploaded for a routine tax payment. This is a payment against an existing Vahan record, not a fresh application.

Steps: pay Kerala road tax online on Vahan

  1. Start on the official portal. Type parivahan.gov.in yourself, open Online Services, then Vehicle Related Services. Do not pay through a lookalike site that adds its own charge.
  2. Select Kerala and your RTO, the RTO where the vehicle is registered, and proceed.
  3. Choose Pay Your Tax (also labelled Pay Vehicle Tax in some menus). This is separate from challan payment.
  4. Enter the registration number and validate with the last 5 digits of the chassis number.
  5. Complete OTP verification on the registered mobile number to open the vehicle details.
  6. Check the details on screen: owner name, vehicle class, fuel, registration date, and the tax paid upto date. If the class or paid upto date is wrong, stop and get the record corrected at the RTO instead of paying against a wrong record.
  7. Select the tax mode: the period Vahan offers for your vehicle, for example quarterly, yearly, monthly, or a one-time or multi-year period. A private lifetime vehicle will not normally show a routine quarterly option.
  8. Review the fee panel total. It shows the tax for the chosen period plus any arrears the system has computed for a late period, and it is final. If it is far from what you expected, confirm it with the RTO before paying.
  9. Pay through the gateway and do not close the window or press back while the bank page is processing.
  10. Download the receipt and keep a copy with the vehicle documents. It is your proof of payment.

How to check the tax due before paying

The same flow doubles as the check. Up to the fee panel you have paid nothing: the details screen shows the tax paid upto position, and the fee panel shows what is due for the period you select. If no tax is due for a private vehicle, its lifetime period is usually still running. If the Vahan record and your last receipt disagree, take both to the RTO.

If the payment is pending or the receipt is lost

If money left your bank but the portal shows the transaction as pending, do not pay again straight away. On Vahan, use Status and the re-verify payment transactions option with your transaction or application number, so the portal can confirm the bank result. If you closed the window without saving the receipt, use Status and then the reprint receipt option: search by application number, receipt number, bank transaction number, or registration number, and download it again. A traffic challan is a different record, and paying tax does not clear it: challans follow the route on our e-challan payment page.

Late payment: what can be verified, and what cannot

Be careful with exact penalty percentages on private sites: they conflict with each other, and I could not verify a current Kerala additional tax or interest table for late road tax on parivahan.gov.in or mvd.kerala.gov.in for this post (both were unreachable at research time in October 2026). The practical position is certain: Vahan adds whatever additional amount applies to a late period into the fee panel total, and that total is what brings the record current. If arrears run over several quarters or years, confirm the total at the RTO before paying, and ask whether any current settlement scheme applies, rather than relying on an old scheme reported in the news.

Paying offline instead

You can still pay at the RTO where the vehicle is registered. Carry the RC and the last tax receipt if you have it. The counter computes the tax from the same Vahan record and issues a receipt. If someone offers to pay for you, treat any amount above the Vahan total as a service charge and ask for the official receipt, not just their own bill.

FAQs

Can I pay Kerala road tax fully online?

Yes, for a tax period that is due on an existing Vahan record. Open Vehicle Related Services on parivahan.gov.in, select Kerala, choose Pay Your Tax, validate with the registration number and the last 5 digits of the chassis number, verify the OTP, choose the tax mode, pay, and save the receipt.

Do private car and bike owners pay road tax every year in Kerala?

No. A new private vehicle pays lifetime tax for 15 years once, at registration. After 15 years, tax is paid for 5 years at a time with re-registration. Yearly or quarterly payment is mainly a transport vehicle pattern.

What is the tax mode on the Vahan payment screen?

It is the period you are paying for: quarterly, yearly, monthly, or a one-time or multi-year period, depending on what Vahan offers for your vehicle class. Pick the period that matches your due date, and check the total before confirming.

How do I know how much tax I owe?

Only Vahan can tell you for your vehicle, because the amount is computed from its class, weight or seating record, price record, and paid upto date. Run the Pay Your Tax flow up to the fee panel to see the due total before you pay.

I paid but did not get the receipt. What now?

Use the reprint receipt option under Status on the Vahan portal, searching by registration number, application number, receipt number, or bank transaction number. If the payment itself shows as pending, use the re-verify payment option first and do not pay again until it resolves.

Does paying road tax also clear my e-challans or green tax?

No. Road tax, traffic e-challans, and green tax are three separate records with separate payments, even though all of them sit around the same vehicle record. Pay each one on its own service and keep each receipt.

Fees, tax rates and rules change without notice. Verify all amounts on parivahan.gov.in before paying: the total Vahan shows for your vehicle is the final figure.
