How to check driving licence status online in Kerala

Last updated: October 2026

You can check a Kerala driving licence application status free of charge on the Sarathi Parivahan portal, sarathi.parivahan.gov.in. Open the portal, choose Kerala as the state, click Application Status, and enter your application number, your date of birth and the captcha shown on screen. The result shows the stage your application has reached, from submission and document verification through testing, approval and dispatch of the card. No RTO visit and no fee are needed for the check itself, and you can repeat it as often as you like.

There are two different checks people mix up. The application status tracks a request you have already filed, such as a new licence, a renewal, a duplicate or an added vehicle class, and it works with the application number from your acknowledgement. The licence status check is for a licence that already exists: it confirms whether the licence is active, when it expires and which vehicle classes it covers, and it works with the DL number. This guide covers both.

What you need before you start

CheckDetails you needWhere the number is
Application statusApplication number, date of birth, captchaOn the acknowledgement slip and the SMS sent when you applied
Existing licence detailsDL number, date of birthPrinted on the licence card itself

Keep the date of birth in the same format you used in the application. Most failed checks are simple mismatches: a wrong digit in the application number, or a date of birth entered in a different order than the form expects. If the portal returns no record, re-enter both values slowly before assuming the application is missing.

Steps to check your application status on Sarathi

  1. Open the official portal. Go to sarathi.parivahan.gov.in yourself by typing the address. Do not follow a status link sent by SMS or a messaging app.
  2. Select Kerala. On the state selection page, choose Kerala so you reach the Kerala Sarathi dashboard.
  3. Click Application Status. It sits in the top menu of the dashboard and opens the status form directly.
  4. Enter the application number. Copy it exactly from your acknowledgement, including every digit.
  5. Enter your date of birth and the captcha. Use the format shown beside the field, then solve the captcha image.
  6. Submit and read the stage list. The screen lists each stage of your application with its current state, so you can see the last completed step and the step still pending.

The same acknowledgement number tracks every licence service, so the steps above also work after a renewal or a duplicate application. If your renewal is the pending item, our Kerala driving licence renewal fees guide explains the fee and the medical certificate rules that apply from age 40.

How to check an existing licence with the DL number

If the card is already issued and you only want to confirm that it is active and in date, use the licence details check instead. On parivahan.gov.in, open Online Services and then Driving Licence Related Services, select Kerala, and use the Know Your Driving Licence Details option. Enter the DL number and date of birth and the portal shows the holder name, the issuing office, the vehicle classes covered and the validity dates for non-transport and transport categories. The mParivahan app shows the same record when you search by DL number, and a licence fetched into DigiLocker carries the same validity dates as the card.

Check the validity dates carefully. A licence can be active as a record and still be expired for driving. If the non-transport validity has passed, you need a renewal rather than a status fix, and the DL renewal service page lists what that application involves. If the address on the record is an old one, correct it with the process in our driving licence address change guide before the next card is printed and posted.

The exact wording of stages varies a little between services, but the order does not: submission, verification, test where one applies, approval, printing, dispatch. If your stage has not moved for a long period while later stages normally follow within days, the remark column on the same screen is the first place to look, because pending objections are recorded there rather than sent separately.

If the status is stuck, rejected or the card has not arrived

Work through the common causes in order. First, a pending fee payment or an incomplete upload leaves the application sitting at an early stage, and the status screen shows that stage as incomplete; finish it from the same dashboard rather than filing a second application. Second, a rejected document shows as a verification remark; correct only the named item. Third, if the card shows dispatched but has not reached you, check that the address on the application is complete and current, and allow for postal delivery time before reporting it undelivered at the RTO. Filing a duplicate while the original is in the post creates two live applications, so confirm the dispatch outcome first; our learner licence service page is the starting point if the stuck item is a first learner application instead.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a fee to check driving licence status online?

No. The status check on Sarathi Parivahan is a free information service. You pay only for the licence service itself, such as issue, renewal or a duplicate, and that payment is made inside the application, never on the status page. A site that asks for payment just to show a status is not the official portal.

Can I check the status without the application number?

Not for an application. The application status form needs the application number with your date of birth. If you have lost the acknowledgement, the number was also sent by SMS at the time of application. For an already issued licence, use the DL number check described above instead.

How long after the driving test does the status change?

There is no single fixed Kerala timeline published for each stage, so treat the status screen itself as the record. The test result stage is recorded by the RTO after your test, and approval, printing and dispatch follow it. If nothing has changed after a reasonable wait, check the remarks on the status screen and then ask at the RTO where you tested, carrying your acknowledgement.

Can I drive while the status shows approved but the card has not arrived?

Fetch the licence into DigiLocker or mParivahan once the record exists and carry that digital copy, which shows the same validity as the card. If no licence record exists yet, the application is still in process and the approval stage alone is not a licence.

Does the same page track renewal and duplicate applications?

Yes. Every Sarathi application, new licence, renewal, duplicate, added class or address change, is tracked with its own application number on the same Application Status page.

Portals and stages change without notice. Verify the current process on parivahan.gov.in and sarathi.parivahan.gov.in before applying, and treat the status shown for your own application number as the final record.
