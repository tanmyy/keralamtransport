Vintage vehicle registration in Kerala: 50-year rule, VA plates, fees and renewal

Last updated: October 2026

If your two-wheeler or car is more than 50 years old from its first registration and still in its original form, you can apply for vintage vehicle registration under the Central Motor Vehicles (Fifteenth Amendment) Rules, 2021. A fresh vintage registration in Kerala gets a VA series plate in the form KL VA AA 0001, the official fee is Rs.20,000 for a new registration and Rs.5,000 for a subsequent re-registration, the first certificate lasts 10 years and renewals run for 5 years at a time, and the vehicle may be used only for restricted heritage purposes, not for daily or commercial use. A vehicle that is already registered keeps its old number: only a fresh registration gets a new VA mark.

Does your vehicle qualify as vintage?

The test is age plus originality, not age alone. The 2021 rules define a vintage motor vehicle as a two-wheeler in categories L1 or L2, or a four-wheeler in category M1, for non-commercial and personal use, that is more than 50 years old from the date of its first registration after its first sale, including an imported vehicle, and that has been maintained in its original form without any substantial overhaul. A substantial overhaul includes a modification to the chassis, the body shell or the engine. Use the date on the first registration record when you check eligibility.

What the VA plate means in Kerala

For a fresh registration, the registration mark follows the pattern XX VA YY followed by a four-digit number from 0001 to 9999, allotted by the State Registering Authority. XX is the state code, so a Kerala fresh registration uses KL, VA stands for vintage, YY is a two-letter series, and the number is the serial in that series. KL VA AA 0001 is shown here only as a format example, not as a number that has been allotted to any vehicle. A vehicle that is already registered as vintage keeps its original registration number and plate intact, with no change.

How to apply, step by step

  1. Confirm the first registration date and the original-form condition from the old registration certificate.
  2. Gather the documents in the table above.
  3. Apply in Form 20 through the Parivahan portal where the vintage route is shown, or at the Kerala registering authority for your area if it is not visible. The 2021 framework provides for a nodal officer to process applications and a committee to inspect a vehicle and declare whether it is fit for vintage registration.
  4. Present the vehicle for inspection of the chassis, body shell and engine against the original form.
  5. Pay Rs.20,000 for a new vintage registration, or Rs.5,000 for a subsequent re-registration.
  6. Wait for the certificate of registration in Form 23A, which the Ministry summary states is issued within 60 days.
  7. Fit the allotted plate if yours is a fresh registration. If your vehicle already had a registration, keep the old number and plate.

What you cannot do with a vintage vehicle

A vintage motor vehicle must not be driven for regular purposes or used for any commercial purpose. The permitted uses are narrow: display, technical research, taking part in a vintage car rally, driving to and from an exhibition or rally, and driving for refuelling and maintenance. Driving to work, running errands, hiring the vehicle out, or using it as a regular family car falls outside that list, even if the vehicle is roadworthy and insured.

Sale, transfer and renewal

Sale and purchase is allowed, but it is not a private handover with no paperwork. The buyer and seller must inform their respective State Transport Authorities, registration under the new owner's name must be recorded, and both parties must jointly submit that information within 90 days of the sale or purchase. For renewal, apply before the certificate runs out: 10 years first, then 5 years at a time, at Rs.5,000. Do not confuse this with the ordinary 15-year cycle: vehicle re-registration after 15 years in Kerala is a different process with different fees and a fitness test. If a vehicle reaches the end of its useful life instead, the separate route is vehicle scrapping in Kerala.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply when my car turns 50?

No. The rule requires more than 50 years from first registration, so a vehicle that has just completed 50 years is not yet eligible. Wait until it is more than 50 years old on that count.

Can I keep my old KL number if I apply for vintage registration?

If the vehicle is already registered, the rules say it keeps its original registration number and plate intact. A new VA mark applies to a fresh registration, such as an imported vehicle being registered for the first time in India.

Can I use a vintage car for a wedding hire or a film shoot for payment?

No. Commercial use is barred for vintage vehicles, including paid hire. A display or rally appearance that fits the permitted list is different from a paid commercial booking.

What happens if my vehicle fails the originality inspection?

It is not declared fit for vintage registration. You would remain on the ordinary registration route unless you restore the vehicle to its original form and apply again.

Can I sell a vintage vehicle?

Yes, with the 90-day joint notice described above, and registration recorded under the new owner's name.

Does vintage registration replace the ordinary fitness certificate?

Not as a blanket rule that could be verified here. The vintage inspection checks originality, not every other certificate your vehicle may need. Ask the registering authority which certificates apply before you drive to an event.

Fees and rules change without notice. Verify all fees and rules on parivahan.gov.in before applying, and treat the fee total shown on your own Vahan application as the final figure.
