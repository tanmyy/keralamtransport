Transport vehicle permit renewal in Kerala

Last updated: November 2026

A transport vehicle permit in Kerala is valid for 5 years, and renewal must be applied for at least 15 days before expiry under Section 81 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988. You can renew online through the Vahan permit service or in person at the issuing RTO. Renewal needs a valid fitness certificate and proof of the current quarter's tax. One gap to know first: no Kerala permit renewal fee table could be verified on an official page in this research, so the fee total on your Vahan payment screen is final, and the figures in the fee table below are guide-reported, not official Kerala rates.

How long a permit lasts, and the renewal deadline

Section 81 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 sets the rules for every ordinary permit, other than a temporary permit under Section 87 or a special permit under Section 88(8).

PointWhat Section 81 says
Validity5 years from the date of issue or renewal
When to applyNot less than 15 days before the permit expires
Late applicationThe authority may accept it if satisfied you had good and sufficient cause for the delay
Renewal after expiryTakes effect from the date of expiry, so there is no gap in validity once it is renewed

Five years is the legal maximum; Kerala guides note the vehicle's age can restrict the validity granted. Check the expiry date printed on your own permit and count 15 days back.

Which permits this guide covers

This renewal route covers the permits a transport vehicle needs to ply for hire or reward. A private car used only for personal trips does not need one.

  • Stage carriage permit: buses picking up passengers at separate fares on a fixed route, with an approved timetable and fare, granted in Kerala by the Chairman of the State Transport Authority.
  • Contract carriage permit: taxis, motor cabs (up to six passengers excluding the driver), maxi cabs and tourist vehicles hired as a whole; valid for 5 years, for the whole state.
  • Goods carriage permit: trucks and other goods vehicles, including a national permit for four or more states; guides put the national permit age cap at 12 years from first registration.
  • Private service vehicle permit: a vehicle used to carry people in connection with the owner's own trade or business, rather than for hire.

Documents you need for renewal in Kerala

The list depends on the permit type. For a stage carriage renewal in Kerala, guides list the fullest set, and the RTO asks for the originals: the existing permit and route permit book in original, the RC, a valid fitness certificate, tax proof for the current quarter, valid insurance, a PUC certificate, a financier NOC if the vehicle is on hire purchase, lease or hypothecation, green tax where applicable, the RTA resolution for the route, a parking area certificate with a recent map and Form F of the parking land, a tenant NOC where the parking land belongs to a tenant, and Aadhaar, address and age proof.

For a contract carriage renewal it is shorter: RC, fitness certificate, tax proof for the current quarter, insurance, financier NOC if the vehicle is on loan, PUC, green tax where applicable, Aadhaar and address proof. Two items cause most delays. The fitness certificate must be valid on the day you apply, so renew it first if it has expired; see our fitness certificate renewal guide. Tax for the current quarter must be paid; see our guide to paying Kerala road tax online. Green tax applies to older transport vehicles at fitness renewal; see our green tax guide.

How to renew online on Vahan

Kerala permit renewals run through the Vahan online permit service, following the documented Vahan flow; the screens your vehicle shows are final, because the services enabled differ by state, RTO and permit category.

  1. Open the Vahan online permit service at vahan.parivahan.gov.in and choose the permit services option.
  2. Select Kerala and your RTO, enter the registration number and the chassis digits the portal asks for, and complete the OTP or registered-mobile authentication.
  3. Choose Renewal of Permit and check the owner, vehicle, permit and insurance details shown.
  4. Upload the supporting documents through the portal's document facility, where the service allows it.
  5. Clear tax and permit dues shown on the portal before or during renewal.
  6. Pay the renewal fee online, review the amount Vahan generates, pay through the gateway, and save the acknowledgement and receipt.
  7. Complete any RTO formalities the application asks for. Some renewals still need document verification at the RTO, and stage carriage renewals can include a check of vehicle condition and seating. One Kerala guide describes an appointment where you reach the RTO before 11:30 am with the documents, so follow the instructions your own application generates.
  8. Check the status and download the renewed permit on Vahan after approval. An Akshaya centre can file the application for you, with the originals in hand for scanning.

Renewing at the RTO in person

If the online route is not enabled for your permit, go to the RTO office for your area with the originals. Get the renewal application form at the counter, present it with the renewal fee, and keep the receipt and acknowledgement number. For a stage carriage renewal the authority checks vehicle condition and seating before processing, so present the vehicle if asked. Guides report about 3 working days; that is a guide figure, not an official timeline, so go by the date on your own acknowledgement.

What if the permit has already expired

Missing the 15-day deadline does not automatically end the permit. Section 81(3) lets the authority entertain a late application if satisfied you had good and sufficient cause for the delay, so a late application needs a reason the authority accepts. If granted after expiry, the renewal takes effect from the date of expiry, which closes the gap. While it is pending, a temporary permit under Section 87 can keep the vehicle running, and its fee is refunded once renewal takes effect. Until the renewal or a temporary permit is in place, do not ply the vehicle: Section 66 requires a transport vehicle to be used in a public place only under a permit, and plying without one is an offence under Section 192, with the fine set by the challan.

What can get a renewal refused

Section 81(4) lists the grounds for refusing renewal, and the authority must give you a hearing before refusing: the applicant's financial condition, shown by insolvency or by decrees for payment of debts left unsatisfied for 30 days, and, for stage carriage operation, being punished twice or more within 12 months for plying without paying the tax due, on an unauthorised route, or making unauthorised trips. One more limit applies to stage carriages: no person can hold 10 or more stage carriage permits, on renewal as much as on a new grant. Keeping tax, fitness and insurance current is the simplest way to stay clear of every ground listed.

Frequently asked questions

How early should I apply for permit renewal in Kerala?

At least 15 days before the expiry date on the permit, as Section 81 requires. Applying earlier gives time to fix a lapsed fitness certificate or unpaid tax.

Can I renew a permit after it has expired?

Yes, if the authority accepts you had good and sufficient cause for the delay. The renewal takes effect from the date of expiry, and a temporary permit can cover the vehicle while it is pending.

Does renewal need a fresh fitness test?

Renewal needs a valid fitness certificate, not necessarily a fresh test that day. If it has expired, renew it first, because the permit renewal will not go through without it.
