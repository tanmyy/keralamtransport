How to download driving licence online in Kerala

Last updated: October 2026

You can download your Kerala driving licence online free of charge in three ways, with no RTO visit if your licence record is already approved: print or save the PDF from Sarathi Parivahan with your application number and date of birth, fetch the licence into DigiLocker with your DL number, or create a virtual licence in the mParivahan app. The DigiLocker and mParivahan versions are treated at par with the physical card for checks in Kerala, so do this as soon as your licence is approved, even before the card arrives by post.

Pick the route that matches what you have. A recent applicant has the application number, so Sarathi print is fastest. A holder with only the DL number should use DigiLocker or mParivahan. All three read the same Sarathi record, so names, classes and validity dates should match across them.

There is no separate download fee on any of these routes, and no download charge could be found on a primary fee page in this run, so treat the download itself as free. Only use parivahan.gov.in, sarathi.parivahan.gov.in, digilocker.gov.in and the official mParivahan app, and do not pay a third party site for this. Fees apply to licence services such as a duplicate card or a renewal, not to this download.

Steps to download from Sarathi Parivahan

  1. Open the official portal yourself. Type sarathi.parivahan.gov.in into your browser. Do not follow a download link sent by message, because lookalike sites copy this service.
  2. Select Kerala. Choose Kerala on the state selection screen so you reach the Kerala Sarathi dashboard.
  3. Open Print Driving Licence. In the Driving Licence menu, choose the Print Driving Licence option.
  4. Enter the application number and date of birth. Copy the application number exactly from your acknowledgement slip and enter the date of birth in the format the form shows.
  5. Submit and check the record. Confirm that the name, DL number, vehicle classes and validity dates on screen are correct before you save anything.
  6. Print or save as PDF. Use the print option and save the file as a PDF for your records, or print it on paper if you need a paper copy today.

If the portal shows no record, your application may not be approved yet. Check the stage first with our guide to checking driving licence status online in Kerala, and try the print route again once the status shows approved or issued.

Steps to fetch the licence in DigiLocker

  1. Sign in to DigiLocker. Open digilocker.gov.in or the DigiLocker app and sign in with your Aadhaar linked mobile number and your security PIN, then verify with the OTP.
  2. Go to Issued Documents. Open the Issued Documents section and choose to get a new document.
  3. Search for Driving Licence. Search for Driving Licence under the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways.
  4. Enter your DL number. Type the DL number exactly as printed on the card, tick the consent box, and choose Get Document.
  5. Open the fetched licence. The licence appears in Issued Documents, pulled live from the transport database. Open it and use View PDF to save a copy.
  6. Keep the app copy. At a check, show the licence from inside the app, so it is clear it is a fetched government record.

Steps to create a virtual licence in mParivahan

  1. Install the official app. Install mParivahan from the Play Store or App Store and register with your mobile number.
  2. Open My DL. On the dashboard, open the DL section and choose to add or create a virtual DL.
  3. Enter the DL number and date of birth. Enter both exactly as they appear on the Sarathi record.
  4. Verify with the OTP. Enter the OTP sent to the mobile number linked to the licence record.
  5. Save the virtual licence. The app stores a virtual licence with a QR code that an officer can scan at a check.

Downloading a learner licence

A learner licence prints from Sarathi in a similar way. Use the Print Learner Licence (Form 3) option, enter the application number and date of birth, verify with the OTP if asked, and save the PDF. Keep it for the full learner period, since you need those details again for the driving test.

Is the downloaded licence valid in Kerala?

Yes, when it is shown the right way. A Ministry of Road Transport and Highways advisory reported by the Press Information Bureau on 9 August 2018 asked states to accept a driving licence presented in electronic form through DigiLocker or mParivahan as valid under the Motor Vehicles Act 1988, at par with the certificate issued by the transport authority, under the Information Technology Act 2000. Kerala Police followed with Circular No. 25 of 2018, instructing officers in Kerala to accept documents shown through DigiLocker or mParivahan on the same basis. If a document has to be impounded, the advisory says the action is recorded electronically through eChallan instead.

A loose copy does not get the same treatment. A photo, screenshot or scan of the card is not a fetched DigiLocker or mParivahan record. Keep the Sarathi PDF for your records and for uploads, and show the live app record at a vehicle check.

Download is not a duplicate licence

Downloading gives you a copy of an existing, valid licence. It does not replace a lost card as a card, it does not extend an expired licence, and it does not change any detail on the record. If the physical card is lost, stolen or damaged and you need a new card issued, apply for a duplicate instead: our duplicate driving licence guide for Kerala covers the fee, Form LLD and the steps. Use the download while the duplicate is in process, provided the licence itself is still valid.

Frequently asked questions

Can I drive in Kerala with only the DigiLocker licence?

Yes. A licence fetched in DigiLocker or shown as a virtual licence in mParivahan is accepted at par with the physical card at checks in Kerala. Show it from inside the app, not as a screenshot.

Is there a fee to download the driving licence?

No fee for the download itself could be verified on an official fee page, and the Sarathi print, DigiLocker fetch and mParivahan routes are used as free record views. If any site asks for payment just to show or download the licence, leave it and use the official portals listed above.

Can I download the licence before the card arrives?

Yes, once the application status shows the licence as approved or issued and a DL number exists. The record comes first and the card follows by post, so the digital copy is often available earlier.

What if my licence was issued long ago and does not appear anywhere?

Older licences that were never entered into Sarathi may not fetch in any app. Take the card to the issuing RTO and ask for the record to be brought onto the Sarathi database, then try the download again.

Portals and menu names change without notice. Verify the current process and any fee on parivahan.gov.in and sarathi.parivahan.gov.in before you apply, and treat the record shown for your own DL number as the final version.
