Driving licence endorsement for transport and PSV vehicles in Kerala

Last updated: November 2026

To drive a taxi, auto-rickshaw, bus or lorry for a living in Kerala, your private licence is not enough. You need two additions. First, a transport endorsement adds a transport class such as LMV-TR or HMV to your licence, through a learner's licence for that class, a driving test, and an application on Sarathi. Second, a PSV badge authorises you to drive a public service vehicle carrying passengers for hire, issued by Kerala MVD only after a structured badge test. The official fees are Rs.150 for the learner's licence plus Rs.50 for its test, Rs.300 for the driving test per class, Rs.500 for adding the class, Rs.200 for issue and Rs.200 for the smart card, with the badge itself at Rs.100.

Endorsement and badge: two different things

The endorsement is about the vehicle class on your licence; the badge is about you as a driver of passenger vehicles. Neither replaces the vehicle's own permit.

Transport classes you can add

The class on your licence decides what you may legally drive.

ClassVehicles it coversTypical work
LMV-TRLight motor vehicle, transport, up to 7,500 kgTaxis and light goods
3WTRThree-wheeler, transportAuto-rickshaws
HMVHeavy motor vehicle, goodsLorries and heavy goods carriers
HPMVHeavy passenger motor vehicleBuses and heavy passenger vehicles

Add one class at a time: the fee is per class, and a separate test is needed for each. The class codes Sarathi shows for your licence are the final list. Adding a non-transport class follows the same Form 8 route: see our guide to adding a new vehicle class.

Who can apply

  • Age 20 or older. Section 4(2) of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 says no person under 20 may drive a transport vehicle in a public place.
  • An LMV licence held for at least one year. Section 7(1) bars a transport learner's licence unless the applicant has held a light motor vehicle licence for at least one year.
  • Form 1A medical certificate. A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is mandatory for a transport licence at any age, under Rule 5(1). See our Form 1A guide.
  • Driving school certificate, Form 5. Kerala procedure guides list a Form 5 certificate for transport vehicle candidates, so enrol at a recognised driving school.
  • Class 8 pass, for the PSV badge. National badge guides list a minimum Class 8 pass, with the mark sheet as proof. No Kerala-specific rule could be verified on an official page, so confirm it at your RTO.
  • Police verification, for the PSV badge. Badge guides list a police clearance certificate as a requirement. This is guide-sourced, so ask the RTO which form of verification it wants.

Documents to keep ready

  • Your existing driving licence, with the LMV class held for at least one year
  • Form 1A medical certificate and Form 5 driving school certificate
  • Proof of age and address: Aadhaar, passport or voter ID
  • Class 8 mark sheet, police verification certificate, photographs and an Aadhaar-linked mobile number, for the PSV badge and OTP verification

Steps: add a transport class on Sarathi

  1. Check the entry rules. Be 20 or older, with your LMV licence completed one year. If either fails, the portal rejects the application.
  2. Get Form 1A from a registered medical practitioner, and Form 5 from your driving school.
  3. Open Sarathi, select Kerala, and choose the learner's licence service for the transport class. Pay Rs.150 plus Rs.50.
  4. Pass the learner's licence theory test; our learner licence application guide covers the format and slot booking.
  5. Hold the transport learner's licence for 30 days; no test can be booked before the wait is over. It is valid for 6 months.
  6. Book the driving test on Sarathi and pay Rs.300 per class; our Kerala driving test guide covers the format.
  7. Pass the test, then apply for the addition of the class: on Sarathi this is the Additional Endorsement of Driving Licence (AEDL) service, in Form 8. Pay Rs.500, plus Rs.200 for issue and Rs.200 for the smart card.
  8. Check the classes printed on the new licence, and track the application on Sarathi until it shows approved. Our status check guide explains what each status means.

Steps: get the PSV badge

  1. Hold the transport endorsement for the class you will drive. A PSV badge without the matching transport class does not authorise you to drive that vehicle.
  2. Collect the badge documents from the list above.
  3. Apply on Sarathi or at the RTO. On Sarathi, select Kerala, then Services on DL, and choose the PSV badge option. If it is not shown for your RTO, apply at the RTO counter.
  4. Book the badge test slot and practise on the Kerala MVD mock tests.
  5. Pass the written test and the practical test: the written test is multiple choice on traffic rules, road safety, first aid and passenger handling, and the practical test is judged by an RTO official.
  6. Pay the Rs.100 badge fee plus any service charge shown, and keep the receipt and application number.
  7. Collect the badge and wear it while driving a PSV. Under the Kerala Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989, Rule 12, the badge is inscribed with the word Driver, an identification number and the issuing district. A driver may hold only one badge, worn on the left chest.

Validity and renewal

A transport licence is issued for 5 years at a time under Section 14(2)(a) of the Act, and renewal needs a fresh Form 1A. For the badge, national guides describe a general validity of three years from issue, with renewal before expiry. No Kerala-specific badge validity could be verified on an official page, so go by the expiry printed on your own badge.

What happens if you drive a PSV without a badge

Driving a taxi, auto-rickshaw or bus without the transport class on your licence is driving without a licence for that vehicle: Section 181 sets the fine at Rs.5,000. Driving with the class but without the badge is a separate violation. A national badge guide puts the general penalty under Section 177 at Rs.500 for a first offence and Rs.1,500 for a repeat offence, and reports that the vehicle may be impounded. Treat the badge figures as guide-sourced.

Frequently asked questions

Can I drive a taxi with only an LMV-NT private licence?

No. A private LMV licence covers only a private car. You need the LMV-TR transport endorsement and a PSV badge to carry passengers for hire.

Do I need a badge for an auto-rickshaw too?

Yes. Auto-rickshaws are public service vehicles, so the driver needs the 3WTR transport endorsement and a PSV badge, the same as a taxi or bus driver.

What does PSVA not obtained mean on my licence?

PSVA stands for Public Service Vehicle Authority. The remark means the licence holder has no PSV badge yet, so they are not authorised to drive a commercial passenger vehicle.

How long does the whole process take?

Count the fixed waits: 30 days on the transport learner's licence before the driving test, plus test and processing time. Badge guides put badge issue at two to four weeks after the tests, a guide figure. Go by the dates on your own Sarathi application.
