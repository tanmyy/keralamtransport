Duplicate HSRP plate replacement in Kerala

Last updated: October 2026

If your High Security Registration Plate (HSRP) is lost, stolen, bent, cracked or faded in Kerala, order a replacement only through the authorised HSRP booking portal, using its Replacement or Replacement/Retain/Transfer option. Your registration number does not change, and you do not apply for a new registration. If the plate is lost or stolen, file a police complaint first and keep the copy for the booking. If the plate is damaged but still in your possession, keep it and follow the portal prompt for proof of damage. A local plate shop cannot issue a genuine HSRP, and a fallen plate cannot simply be screwed back on, because its tamper-proof snap locks break on removal. No separate duplicate-specific Kerala fee could be verified on parivahan.gov.in or mvd.kerala.gov.in for this guide, so the total shown on your own portal checkout is the final amount.

The distinction in national replacement guides is this: lost or stolen means a police record, because a missing plate could be misused on another vehicle; damaged and still in your possession means the plate itself is the evidence. If the portal or fitment centre asks for a police copy in your case, follow that instruction and keep a copy of everything you submit.

Details and documents to keep ready

  • Vehicle registration number, exactly as on the RC.
  • Chassis number and engine number, copied from the RC, not from memory. A mismatch with the Vahan record is the most common reason a booking stalls.
  • Registration Certificate (RC), original for fitment and a copy for the booking if asked.
  • Police complaint or FIR copy, for a lost or stolen plate.
  • The damaged plate or its photo, for a damaged plate, if the portal asks for it.
  • The laser-etched code of the old plate, if you have it from the windscreen sticker or an earlier booking receipt.

Steps: book a duplicate HSRP plate replacement in Kerala

  1. Secure the record first. For a lost or stolen plate, file the police complaint and keep the copy. For a damaged plate, keep the plate safe.
  2. Open the authorised portal. Kerala bookings run through the authorised HSRP portals, bookmyhsrp.com and makemyhsrp.com. Our HSRP service page summarises the same booking route in brief.
  3. Choose the replacement option. Select Replacement Booking, or Replacement/Retain/Transfer. A replacement order must match the plate already recorded for your vehicle.
  4. Enter the vehicle details. Select Kerala as the registration state, then enter the registration number, chassis number and engine number and complete the captcha.
  5. Select the reason and upload the proof. Choose lost, stolen or damaged, as the portal words it. Upload the police copy for a lost or stolen plate, or the damaged-plate proof the portal asks for.
  6. Choose the fitment centre and slot. Pick an authorised centre near you. Home fitment, where offered, may be billed as an add-on.
  7. Check the breakup and pay online by UPI, card or net banking, and download the receipt.
  8. Track the order on the same portal with the order number and registration number. Guides put delivery at roughly 3 to 7 working days, but no fixed Kerala timeline could be verified, so the tracking screen is your timeline.
  9. Attend fitment with the RC. The centre rivets the new plate with fresh snap locks. Hand over the old damaged plate if the centre asks for it.
  10. Check the new plate before you leave against the RC, including the hologram, laser code and windscreen sticker if a new one was supplied.

Can you drive while you wait for the replacement?

A booking receipt and a police copy explain your situation at a check, but neither is a number plate, and neither makes driving without a legible plate legal. Limit use of the vehicle until the replacement is fitted, carry both documents when you must move it, for example to the fitment centre, and never use a handwritten, printed or locally made plate as a stand-in.

That is separate from the deadline position for old vehicles that never had HSRP, covered in our HSRP last date and fine guide for Kerala.

Special cases

  • You bought the vehicle second-hand and the RC is not yet in your name: finish the ownership transfer first, then book. See our RC transfer in Kerala guide.
  • The vehicle is from another state: book with the number on the RC. If it has been re-registered in Kerala, use the KL number and the new RC details.
  • Both plates are gone: say so in the police complaint, listing front and rear, and book a full set. Do not book a single plate and hope the second is waived.
  • The snap lock broke and the plate fell off at home: if you still have the plate, treat it as damaged, not reusable. The locks are non-reusable by design.

FAQs

Does my registration number change when I get a duplicate HSRP?

No. A duplicate or replacement HSRP carries the same registration number, linked to the same chassis and engine numbers. Only the physical plate and its laser-etched code are new.

Is an FIR compulsory for a damaged plate?

For a lost or stolen plate, yes. For a plate damaged but still in your possession, national guides say the plate or its photo is the proof, but follow the portal instruction if it asks for a police copy.

Can a local number plate shop make my replacement?

No. A genuine HSRP comes only through the authorised portal and an authorised centre, with snap locks, a hologram and a laser-etched code linked to your vehicle record.

How long does a replacement take in Kerala?

No fixed Kerala timeline could be verified on an official page. Guides commonly put it at about 3 to 7 working days from booking to fitment, depending on the centre. Track your order on the portal and go by its ready-for-fitment status.

What does a duplicate HSRP cost in Kerala?

No separate duplicate-specific Kerala rate could be verified on an official page for this guide. Use the published first-fit maximum rates (Rs.425 two-wheeler, Rs.695 car) only as a reference point, and treat the total and breakup on your own portal checkout as final.

Can I replace only the front plate?

Yes, if only the front plate is lost or damaged, book the replacement for that plate and tell the fitment centre which one it is. If both are affected, book the full set.
