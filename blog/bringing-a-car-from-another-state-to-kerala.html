Bringing a car from another state to Kerala: re-registration and road tax

Last updated: October 2026

If you bring a vehicle registered in another state and keep it in Kerala for more than 12 months, you must apply for a new Kerala registration mark and pay Kerala road tax. That is Section 47 of the Motor Vehicles Act. The sequence is: get a No Objection Certificate on Form 28 from the RTO where the vehicle is registered, file Form 27 at the Kerala RTO, pay the Kerala tax and fee assessed, and take the new KL number and RC. Afterwards you can claim a pro-rata refund of the unused road tax from the state you left. A stay of 12 months or less needs no re-registration.

Do you have to re-register? The 12-month rule

Section 47 is about how long the vehicle is kept in Kerala, not when you bought it. Three cases cover almost everyone:

  • Short visit or posting, 12 months or less: keep the original registration. No NOC, no Kerala tax, no new number. Keep proof of when the vehicle entered Kerala, in case you are asked.
  • Permanent move to Kerala: re-registration is mandatory once the vehicle has been kept here over 12 months. If the move is clearly permanent, apply as soon as you have a Kerala address instead of waiting out the year.
  • You bought a used vehicle in another state: the same re-registration applies, plus an ownership transfer. Add Forms 29 and 30, signed by seller and buyer, to the file below.

Paying some Kerala tax online by itself does not complete this process. The legal step is the assignment of a new mark; tax is assessed as part of that application.

Start in the old state: the Form 28 NOC

Nothing in Kerala can finish until the origin RTO clears the vehicle. Apply there on Form 28, in triplicate, or four copies if the vehicle is under a loan, because the financier's consent must be recorded on it. Under Section 48 the origin RTO checks that tax is paid up to date, that no demand is pending, and obtains a police report that no theft case is reported or pending. It has 30 days to grant the NOC or refuse it in writing, with reasons.

Clear pending challans, unpaid old-state tax and loan paperwork first, because each can hold the NOC up. RTOs commonly treat an issued NOC as usable for about six months, so do not collect it months early. Our guide to the reverse journey, NOC for car transfer from Kerala to another state, covers the same Form 28 paperwork from the Kerala side.

If the NOC has not been issued, Section 47 still lets you file in Kerala with the receipt for your NOC application, or the postal acknowledgement if you applied by registered post, plus a declaration that no refusal was communicated to you. That route is slower, so a granted NOC in hand is better.

Add Rs.200 if the new RC is issued as a smart card. The NOC fee is set by the origin state, not Kerala. These fees are small; the real cost is the Kerala road tax below. Anything a helper charges above this table is a service charge, so ask for an itemised quote.

Kerala road tax on an incoming vehicle: the big cost

Road tax does not transfer between states. Kerala levies its own lifetime tax under the Kerala Motor Vehicles Taxation Act when the new mark is assigned, even if you paid lifetime tax in the old state. Two principles decide the amount:

  • The base is the vehicle's original purchase value, its invoice price when new, not the second-hand price you may have paid for it.
  • That value is reduced for the vehicle's age. The older the vehicle, the smaller the taxable value.

For reference, the current Kerala lifetime slabs for a new private car, the starting point for the assessment, are 10% up to Rs.5 lakh, 13% from Rs.5 to Rs.10 lakh, 15% from Rs.10 to Rs.15 lakh, 17% from Rs.15 to Rs.20 lakh, and 22% above Rs.20 lakh, all on the ex-showroom price. Full tables and worked examples are in our Kerala road tax calculator post.

What I could not verify: no current official Kerala depreciation table for incoming vehicles could be verified on parivahan.gov.in or mvd.kerala.gov.in at research time, because neither site responded. Tables circulating online conflict, and some still use the old 9% to 21% slabs, so do not budget from them. The Vahan fee panel shows the exact tax for your vehicle before you pay; treat that as final. Vehicles over 15 years old will also face fitness and green tax questions.

Documents checklist

  • Original RC of the vehicle
  • Form 28 NOC from the origin RTO, in original
  • Form 27, the application for assignment of a new registration mark
  • Kerala address proof and ID proof (Aadhaar covers both, with an OTP-linked mobile number)
  • PAN card, or Form 60 if there is no PAN
  • Valid insurance certificate and valid PUC certificate
  • Original purchase invoice of the vehicle, where you have it
  • Chassis number pencil print and passport-size photographs
  • Financier NOC, if the vehicle is under hypothecation
  • Forms 29 and 30 as well, if ownership is also changing

Steps: re-register in Kerala

  1. Clear the old record first. Pay pending challans and old-state tax dues, and get the financier NOC if there is a loan.
  2. Get the Form 28 NOC from the origin RTO, ideally before the vehicle leaves that state.
  3. Start on Vahan. Go to parivahan.gov.in, open Vehicle Related Services, select Kerala, enter the registration number, and choose the service for assignment of a new registration mark.
  4. Fill Form 27 and upload documents. Enter the Kerala address exactly as it appears on your address proof.
  5. Check the fee panel and pay. It shows the assignment fee, the smart card charge and the Kerala road tax assessed. Our guide to paying Kerala road tax online covers the Vahan payment screens.
  6. Visit the Kerala RTO with the vehicle. Carry all originals. The RTO verifies the chassis number against the documents and the origin record.
  7. Take the new KL number. The new RC is dispatched to your Kerala address. Fit HSRP plates with the KL number and have the insurance endorsed with the new number and address.
  8. Claim the old-state refund. Apply to the origin RTO with proof of the Kerala re-registration and tax receipt. It covers the unused portion of the old tax and is slow, so budget for paying Kerala tax in full first.

If you do nothing after 12 months

The vehicle stays on the old state's record while living in Kerala. Kerala can demand its tax for the period kept here, and any later sale, insurance claim or transfer starts with this unfinished move. The fix is the Form 27 application itself.

The BH series alternative, and its limit

BH is designed to avoid repeated re-registration for owners who move often, but it is chosen at registration by eligible owners. It is not a conversion for an existing vehicle mid-move, and no official Kerala conversion procedure could be verified. If you are buying new, read our BH series registration in Kerala post first.

FAQs

Can I drive in Kerala on another state's number plate?

Yes, for up to 12 months from when the vehicle is brought into Kerala. Beyond that, Section 47 requires a new Kerala registration mark.

Do I pay road tax twice when I move?

Up front, yes. Kerala does not credit the old state's tax. Your remedy is a pro-rata refund claim for the unused period, filed with the old state after Kerala re-registration.

Can I keep my old registration number?

No. A new mark means a new KL number, a new RC and new HSRP plates.

How long does the NOC take?

The origin RTO has 30 days under Section 48 to grant or refuse the NOC in writing. No fixed official timeline for the Kerala stage could be verified, so track the application on Vahan.

What if I do not have the car's original invoice?

Take whatever purchase papers you have. Where the invoice is missing, the RTO works out the value from its records, and the tax on the Vahan panel is what you pay.
