When to apply for a permanent driving licence after a learner permit in Kerala

Last updated: October 2026

You can apply for a permanent driving licence in Kerala only after holding your learner licence for at least 30 days, and only while it is still valid, which means within 180 days of issue. The Sarathi portal enforces this window: apply a day early and the system will not let you through. The rest of the process is familiar: file the application on sarathi.parivahan.gov.in, pay the fee, book a driving test slot and pass the test at your RTO. Current Sarathi fee guides list the driving test at Rs.300 per vehicle class and permanent licence issuance at Rs.200, but I could not open the live Parivahan fee page while researching, so treat the figure the portal shows at checkout as final.

The 30-day rule: count from the issue date

The Motor Vehicles Rules require a 30-day gap between the issue of the learner licence and the application for a permanent licence. The purpose is straightforward: one month of supervised practice before anyone attempts the test. A few worked examples make the counting clear:

  • Learner licence issued on 1 October: earliest application date is 31 October.
  • Learner licence issued on 15 March: earliest application date is 14 April.
  • If you apply through a driving school, they will count for you, but it is worth checking yourself, because a rejected application wastes the slot-booking effort.

This is a minimum, not a recommendation. Most first-time riders in Kerala need several weeks of practice anyway, since the state is known for one of the toughest driving tests in India. Our guide to applying for a learner licence in Kerala covers the documents and fee you paid at the LL stage.

The 180-day window: do not let the learner licence expire

A learner licence is valid for six months from its date of issue. You must apply for the permanent licence within those 180 days. If the learner licence expires before you pass the test, you start the learner process again and the 30-day clock restarts. If you are nearing expiry and cannot get a test slot, our guide to booking driving test slots in Kerala explains what to do when slots show as full.

Official fees for the permanent licence

Fee itemAmount
Driving test, per vehicle classRs.300 (shown in current Sarathi fee guides; confirm on the payment screen)
Issue of permanent driving licenceRs.200 (shown in current Sarathi fee guides; confirm on the payment screen)
Smart card feeRs.200 where charged separately; some RTOs fold it into the issue fee

Old Kerala fee pages quote Rs.50 for the driving test and Rs.200 flat for the licence; those figures come from an old notification and are outdated. If a driving school or agent quotes a package of several thousand rupees, that is their service charge for training, vehicle use on test day and paperwork, not the official fee. Ask for an itemised quote before you pay.

Documents you will need

  • Form 4: the application for a permanent driving licence (generated online on Sarathi; at a counter, it is the form you fill and sign).
  • Original learner licence: issued in Kerala and still valid, held for at least 30 days.
  • Age proof: Aadhaar, passport, birth certificate or Class 10 certificate.
  • Address proof: Aadhaar, passport, voter ID, ration card or utility bill.
  • Passport-size photographs: as required by the RTO counter or the online form.
  • Form 1A medical certificate: required if you are over 40 years old; for commercial (transport) vehicle classes in all cases.

Steps to apply on Sarathi

  1. Open sarathi.parivahan.gov.in and choose Driving Licence Related Services.
  2. Select Kerala as the state.
  3. Choose Apply for New Driving Licence (the new-DL service, not the learner licence one).
  4. Enter your learner licence number and date of birth. The portal checks the 30-day and 180-day conditions automatically.
  5. Fill in the application, upload the documents and select the vehicle classes you want tested in.
  6. Pay the fee online. Keep the receipt with the application number.
  7. Book a driving test slot. Slots open a few weeks ahead and popular RTOs fill fast.
  8. Attend the test at the RTO on the scheduled date with the receipt, your learner licence and the vehicle for the test (your own or the driving school's).

If your name, photograph or address changed since the learner licence was issued, fix the learner licence details first; mismatches between the LL and the new application are a common reason for rejection. For the test itself, our learner licence test question bank is still worth a refresher, because examiners in Kerala ask about signs and rules during the practical too.

What the Kerala driving test involves

Kerala tests three skills: an 8-shaped course for two-wheelers, the H track for four-wheelers, and a short road test. The automated test tracks at some RTOs score you with sensors; at manual RTOs an inspector judges. If you fail, you book a fresh slot and the test fee applies to each new attempt, so arrive early, check your vehicle's documents and practice the exact manoeuvres, not just general driving.

FAQs

Can I apply for the permanent licence on day 30 of my learner licence?

Yes, once 30 full days have passed from the issue date. Applying earlier will be rejected by the portal, and at a counter the clerk will send you back.

Does the 180-day validity include the test date?

The application must be made while the learner licence is valid. Keep a margin: book your test well before the last weeks of the learner licence, because if the licence expires mid-process you will need a fresh learner licence.

Can I add a second vehicle class at the permanent licence stage?

Yes. You can apply for multiple classes (for example two-wheeler and car) in one permanent licence application; the test fee applies per class. The application form and slot booking let you select the classes.

How long does the permanent licence take after passing?

Once you pass, the licence is approved by the RTO and posted to your registered address. The card arrives by post and a digital copy is available through DigiLocker or the mParivahan app.

Do I need to surrender my learner licence?

No separate surrender step is needed. The learner licence is superseded once the permanent licence is issued.

What if I fail the driving test?

You can rebook another slot. There is a short cooling period before a retest in most states, so check the slot calendar. Practise the manoeuvres you failed on before the new date.
