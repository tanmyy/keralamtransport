Name correction in RC in Kerala

Last updated: October 2026

If the owner name on your vehicle Registration Certificate (RC) is wrong, the correction is made on the same owner's record: it is not an ownership transfer, and the registration number does not change. In Kerala you start on Vahan through parivahan.gov.in, under Vehicle Related Services, using the RC particulars or correction route, proving the correct name with a government ID that already carries it. If the portal cannot take your case, the registering RTO makes the correction after verifying your originals. No separate name correction fee could be verified on the official Parivahan fee schedule: where a corrected smart card is issued, the charge that applies is the Rs.200 smart card charge, and the total shown on your own Vahan application is the final amount.

First, work out which case yours is

The documents depend on why the name is wrong. Three cases cover almost everyone.

Your caseWhat it isCore proof
Spelling or typing errorThe name was entered wrongly at registration, often from the dealer's entry, while your ID was always spelled the other wayID proof with the correct spelling, plus the sale invoice if the error started there
Legal name changeYou changed your name in law, so your ID documents now carry a new name that the RC has never shownGazette notification of the change, an affidavit, and ID in the new name
Name change after marriage or divorceYour surname or name changed with a change in marital statusMarriage certificate, or the divorce decree where that is the basis, plus ID in the new name

In all three cases the owner stays the same person. If the vehicle was sold to a different person, that is an ownership transfer on Forms 29 and 30, not a correction.

Fee for a name correction in the RC

The central fee schedule in Rule 81 lists registration, renewal, duplicate RC and transfer fees, but as last read for this site it carries no separate line for a name correction in an RC. That differs from a driving licence, where a change of particulars has its own Rs.200 fee.

ItemPosition
Separate name correction fee on the official fee scheduleNo separate line verified

Verification gap: parivahan.gov.in and mvd.kerala.gov.in did not respond at research time in October 2026, so the fee position is based on the Rule 81 schedule as last read for this site and reproduced by state transport departments, with the live Vahan payment screen treated as final. Secondary guides describe only a small total (applicant discussions put a corrected smart card at around Rs.200 to Rs.300): those are reported amounts, not a verified official fee. Anyone helping you file is charging a service charge on top, so ask for an itemised receipt.

Documents to keep ready

  • Original RC, the smart card where one was issued
  • Government ID proof carrying the correct name, such as Aadhaar, passport, PAN or voter ID, self-attested
  • Valid insurance certificate and valid PUC certificate
  • Sale invoice of the vehicle, for a spelling error that began at the dealer entry
  • For a legal name change: the gazette notification and an affidavit of the change
  • For marriage or divorce: the marriage certificate or divorce decree that supports the new name
  • An affidavit stating that the name on the RC and the correct name are one and the same person, where the RTO asks for one (applicant reports say this is common for spelling corrections)

Update your core ID first: the RTO corrects the RC to match the proof you show. The RC, insurance and licence names should end up identical.

Steps: apply on Vahan

  1. Open the official portal. Go to parivahan.gov.in, open Online Services, then Vehicle Related Services, and select Kerala.
  2. Enter the registration number and proceed to the RTO where the vehicle is registered.
  3. Open the RC particulars or correction service for the vehicle. Menu labels change, so follow the closest label.
  4. Verify the vehicle. Enter the chassis details the portal asks for and complete the OTP or Aadhaar based authentication where shown.
  5. Enter the correct name exactly as on your ID proof, character by character, including initials and their order.
  6. Upload the documents for your case from the list above, in clear scans.
  7. Check the fee panel and pay the total shown, if the portal asks for payment, and save the acknowledgement and application number.
  8. Attend the RTO if asked for verification, with the original RC and originals of everything uploaded.
  9. Track the application on Vahan with the application number until the record is approved and the corrected RC is dispatched to your address on record.

If the portal cannot take it: the RTO route

  1. Go to the RTO where the vehicle is registered, with the original RC and the full document set for your case.
  2. Write a short application on plain paper naming the vehicle, the name as printed on the RC, the correct name, and the reason: spelling error, legal change, marriage or divorce.
  3. Submit the application and documents, show the originals for verification, and pay the amount the counter or the generated application shows. Keep the receipt and any inward or application number.
  4. Collect or receive the corrected RC, and check the name spelling before you leave or as soon as the card arrives.

This route suits old or partly digitised records and gazette cases an officer has to examine. If only the address is wrong, use the Form 33 route instead: see our guide to change of address in the RC in Kerala.

After the correction: update the copies that still show the old name

  • Insurance: ask your insurer to endorse the corrected name on the policy. A mismatch between the RC and the policy is a common reason claims get questioned.
  • Digital RC: fetch a fresh copy after the record updates, using our guide to downloading your RC from DigiLocker and mParivahan. The old saved copy still shows the old name.
  • Driving licence: the licence is a separate Sarathi record and does not update with the RC. If the same name is wrong there, use our guide to how to change name in driving licence in Kerala, where the official fee for a change of particulars is Rs.200.
  • Loan record: if the vehicle is under hypothecation, tell the financier, so a later hypothecation removal does not stall on a mismatch.
  • Check the live record on the Vahan lookup in our guide to checking vehicle registration details online in Kerala before relying on it for a sale or claim.

Frequently asked questions

Is a name correction in the RC the same as an ownership transfer?

No. A correction keeps the same owner and number. A transfer moves the vehicle to a different person, on Forms 29 and 30, at half the registration fee (Rs.150 for a two-wheeler, Rs.300 for a car, plus Rs.200 for a smart card).

Do I need a gazette notification to fix a spelling mistake?

For a pure spelling error, where your ID already carries the correct name, applicant guides treat ID proof and, where asked, a one and the same person affidavit as the core papers, not a gazette. A gazette belongs to a legal change to a new name. No Kerala circular fixing one document list for every RC correction could be verified, so carry the fuller set for your case.

Can I correct the name in the RC fully online in Kerala?

Start online on Vahan in every case. Spelling corrections on digitised records are the most likely to finish with an OTP or Aadhaar check, while gazette cases and older records commonly end with an RTO verification visit.

Will my registration number change?

No. The number, the vehicle details and the registration validity stay as they are. Only the owner name on the record and on the reissued card changes.

How long does an RC name correction take in Kerala?

No fixed official timeline could be verified, and neither official site responded at research time. Track the application number on Vahan, and do not rely on the corrected name until the live record shows it.

My RC is lost as well as wrongly named. What then?

A duplicate reprints the record as it stands, errors included, so correct the record first where you can. Duplicate fees and steps are in our guide to duplicate RC fees in Kerala.

Fees and rules change without notice. Verify all fees and rules on parivahan.gov.in before applying, and treat the fee total shown on your own Vahan application as the final figure.
