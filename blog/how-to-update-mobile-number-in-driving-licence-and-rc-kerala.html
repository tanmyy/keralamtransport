How to update the mobile number linked to your driving licence and vehicle registration

Last updated: October 2026

The direct answer: your driving licence and your vehicle registration are two separate records, and the mobile number has to be updated in each one separately. Update the licence record on Sarathi Parivahan, using Others and then Mobile Number Update, and update the vehicle record on Vahan. There is no fee for a mobile number update on either portal, and the update does not reprint your card. Both routes verify you with an OTP, in most cases sent to your Aadhaar linked mobile, so keep that phone with you. If the online check fails, the registering RTO in Kerala is the fallback.

Why one update is not enough

Parivahan sends almost every OTP to the number already on the record, so an old number blocks online services, not just reminders.

RecordPortalWhat breaks when the number is old

Updating one record does not update the other. If you hold a licence and own a vehicle, do both updates, then test each record before you need it urgently.

The mobile number is a record field, not a printed card field, so no reprint is needed. Anyone charging for this update is selling assisted filing: treat that amount as a service charge and ask for an itemised receipt.

The same new number can be used for both records, and for more than one vehicle. If the mobile linked to your Aadhaar is itself old, update that with UIDAI first, or use the RTO route below.

Steps: update the number on your driving licence on Sarathi

  1. Open Sarathi Parivahan. Go to sarathi.parivahan.gov.in, or through parivahan.gov.in and Driving Licence Related Services.
  2. Select Kerala as the state, if the licence was issued in Kerala.
  3. Open Others, then Mobile Number Update. This is a standalone service: you do not start a renewal or duplicate application to change the number.
  4. Choose the licence category, such as driving licence or learner licence, and enter the licence number and date of birth.
  5. Complete the Aadhaar verification the screen asks for, using the OTP sent to your Aadhaar linked mobile.
  6. Check the record shown. Confirm the name and licence details are yours before going further.
  7. Enter the new mobile number carefully, generate the OTP to that new number where the portal asks for it, and enter it to confirm.
  8. Submit and save the confirmation and the confirmation SMS on the new number.

After the update, our driving licence status check guide is a quick test of the new number.

Steps: update the number on your vehicle registration on Vahan

  1. Open Vahan. Go to vahan.parivahan.gov.in and open the mobile number update service, or go through parivahan.gov.in, Vehicle Related Services, select Kerala and your RTO, and find Update Mobile Number under Services or Additional Services.
  2. Enter the vehicle details exactly as on the RC: registration, chassis and engine numbers, registration date and validity date.
  3. Choose Show Details and check that the vehicle and owner details shown match your RC.
  4. Enter the owner details for verification: owner name as per Aadhaar and the Aadhaar linked mobile, and tick the consent box where shown.
  5. Verify with the OTP sent to the Aadhaar linked mobile.
  6. Enter the new mobile number where the portal asks for it, confirm it, and submit.
  7. Save the success message. The Vahan record now carries the new number for OTP services and alerts.

Menu labels change from time to time: follow the label closest to Update Mobile Number, and stay only on official parivahan.gov.in domains. Our guide to checking vehicle registration details online in Kerala shows the same Vahan record.

After both updates: test and refresh your copies

Do two quick checks. First, use an OTP based Parivahan service for each record and confirm the code now arrives on the new number. Second, fetch fresh digital copies after the records update, using our guides to downloading a driving licence online in Kerala and downloading your RC from DigiLocker and mParivahan. This update does not change your address or name: those are separate services, for example the driving licence address change.

If the online update fails: the RTO route

Go to the RTO or sub RTO that issued the licence or registered the vehicle, with the original licence or RC, Aadhaar and another ID proof. Ask for the number to be corrected on the Sarathi or Vahan record and, before you leave, test it with an OTP based service. Use this route when the Aadhaar details do not match, when the record is an old paper record the portal cannot fetch, or when you no longer have the Aadhaar linked mobile. No separate official fee for an RTO assisted correction could be verified on an official page: if a charge is shown, ask what service it is for and keep the receipt.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a fee to update the mobile number on a driving licence or RC in Kerala?

No. No fee for a mobile number update is listed on the Parivahan fee pages for either record, and the update does not reprint a card. If a payment screen appears, check which service you have actually opened before paying.

Does updating the DL number update the vehicle number automatically?

No. Sarathi and Vahan are separate databases, so update each record separately.

Can I update the number without Aadhaar?

The normal online route uses Aadhaar OTP verification, so in practice you need access to the Aadhaar linked mobile. Without it, visit the issuing RTO with your original documents and ask for the record to be corrected there.

How long does the update take?

The portal update completes on screen once the OTP steps succeed, with a confirmation to the new number. No fixed official timeline could be verified, so test the record the same day with an OTP service.

Will I get a new licence card or RC with the new number?

No, and you do not need one. What changes is the number stored against the record, which is what OTPs and alerts use.

Fees and portal screens change without notice. Verify the current process on parivahan.gov.in before you start, and treat the live Sarathi and Vahan screens for your own licence and vehicle numbers as the final record.
