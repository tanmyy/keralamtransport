Driving licence extract in Kerala

Last updated: October 2026

A driving licence extract in Kerala is a certified history of your driving licence, issued through Sarathi Parivahan and your Kerala RTO. It is not a new licence and it does not let you drive. It is a record document that lists your licence number, the date your licence was first issued, the vehicle classes you hold, validity, renewals and endorsements, in a form that a foreign licensing authority, an embassy, an employer or an insurer can verify. Most people ask for one when converting an Indian licence abroad or building a visa file, because the card alone often does not show the first issue date or full history.

What a DL extract shows

The extract is drawn from your Sarathi record, so a detail missing or wrong there will be missing or wrong on the extract too. Get the record corrected first.

FieldWhy it matters
Licence number and holder nameMatches the extract to your licence card and passport
Date of first issueForeign authorities use this to count your years of driving experience
Vehicle classes held, with datesShows, for example, when motorcycle and car classes were each added
Validity and renewal historyShows the licence is current and how long it has been held
Issuing RTO and endorsements on recordIdentifies the authority that issued and maintains the record

The first issue date is the most common reason for the request. A renewed card usually shows the renewal date most prominently, and a foreign counter that credits experience from first issue will ask for a separate proof. The extract is that proof.

What a DL extract is not

Three documents are often confused. A DL extract is a history certificate. An International Driving Permit lets you drive abroad for a short visit, covered in our guide to an international driving permit from Kerala. A digital licence in DigiLocker or mParivahan is a copy of the licence itself for traffic checks in India, covered in our guide to how to download a driving licence online in Kerala. Neither proves your first issue date. If the authority asked for an extract, a history certificate or a verification letter, the DL extract is the service you want.

Who usually needs one

  • Foreign licence conversion: licensing authorities in countries such as Canada, Australia and Singapore commonly ask for an extract or an equivalent letter from the issuing authority, especially where the card does not show the first issue date. Singapore, for example, expressly asks for an extract showing the date of first issue where that date is not on the licence itself.
  • Visa and employment files: some employers and visa files ask for a verified licence history for driving jobs.
  • Insurance and legal use: an extract can support a claim or file where your licence history is in question.

Before you apply, read what the requesting authority wants. Some accept the Sarathi print, and some insist on an extract signed and stamped by the RTO. That detail decides whether the online route alone is enough.

Fee for a DL extract in Kerala

There is no separate DL extract fee that I could verify on Parivahan's Licensing Related Fees and Charges page in October 2026, and parivahan.gov.in and mvd.kerala.gov.in did not respond at research time, so I am not presenting any figure below as an official Kerala fee. Treat the amount shown on your own Sarathi application and payment screen as the final fee.

If the payment screen shows a very different total, check that you selected DL Extract and not a duplicate licence by mistake.

Documents to keep ready

  • Your driving licence, original and a clear copy of both sides on one page
  • Aadhaar card or another accepted ID proof
  • The pre-filled application form and payment receipt, if you applied online
  • Your passport, if the extract is for use abroad

Make sure the name on the licence matches your passport. If it does not, fix that first: our guide to how to change name in driving licence in Kerala covers that separate Rs.200 service. A name mismatch is a common reason foreign counters return the file.

How to apply for a DL extract online on Sarathi

  1. Go to sarathi.parivahan.gov.in, open Driving Licence Related Services, and select Kerala as the state.
  2. Open Services on DL, the menu that also covers renewal, duplicate and other services on an existing licence, and click Continue.
  3. Enter your driving licence number and date of birth, and use the DL extract option to pull up your record. Check every detail on screen before you proceed.
  4. Select DL Extract from the list of services, choose the reason for applying, for example foreign licence conversion, and confirm.
  5. Submit with the captcha, and save the acknowledgement. Note the application number: you will need it to track the application and to print the pre-filled form.
  6. Download the pre-filled application form, pay the amount shown on the payment screen if the portal asks for payment, and save the payment receipt.
  7. Follow the instruction on your acknowledgement. The extract may be available as a portal print, or the RTO may verify your documents and issue a signed extract. Carry the documents above if you are asked to attend.
  8. Track the application with the Application Status service on Sarathi, using your application number and date of birth. Our guide to how to check driving licence status online in Kerala explains what each status means.

How to apply at the RTO in person

  1. Go to the RTO that issued your licence, or that now holds your record.
  2. Write a short request on plain paper with your licence number, name, date of birth and reason, and attach a copy of the licence and your ID proof.
  3. Submit the request, show the original licence for verification, and pay the amount the counter quotes. Keep the receipt and any application or inward number you are given.
  4. Collect the signed extract when ready, and check the first issue date, classes and name spelling before you leave.

No fixed Kerala processing timeline could be verified on an official page. Do not plan a foreign appointment around a reported time in a secondary guide. Apply well before any deadline abroad, and keep the acknowledgement.

If your licence record does not show up online

Older paper licences and some early smart cards were not always digitised into Sarathi. If the portal cannot find your record, go to the issuing RTO and ask for the record to be entered or corrected first, with the original licence as proof, then apply again.

FAQs

Is a DL extract the same as a duplicate driving licence?

No. A duplicate licence replaces a lost or damaged card and is itself a licence. An extract is only a history certificate: it proves details about your licence, but it is not a licence and you cannot drive on it.

Can I get a DL extract for a licence issued outside Kerala?

Apply through the state that issued the licence, selecting that state on Sarathi. A Kerala RTO cannot certify the history of a licence issued and held on another state's record.

Does a foreign authority always accept the online print?

Not always. Some require an extract signed and stamped by the RTO. If the request says certified, signed or stamped, use the RTO route.

How long is a DL extract valid?

No official validity period could be verified. It is a snapshot of your record on the issue date, and a requesting authority may ask for a recent one.

Can someone else collect my extract?

No Kerala rule on collection by another person could be verified. If you are already abroad, ask the issuing RTO what it will accept, and give whoever follows up your acknowledgement and application number.

What if the first issue date on my extract is wrong?

Take the original licence and any older renewal papers to the issuing RTO, ask for the Sarathi record to be corrected, then apply for a fresh extract.
