Adding hypothecation to an RC in Kerala

Last updated: October 2026

If you take a vehicle loan after your vehicle is already registered, the financier's name does not appear on your Registration Certificate (RC) automatically. You add it by filing Form 34, signed by both you and the financier, through the Vahan Hypothecation Addition service. The official endorsement fee is Rs.500 for a motorcycle, Rs.1,500 for a three-wheeler, quadricycle or light motor vehicle, and Rs.3,000 for a medium or heavy vehicle, plus Rs.200 where a new smart card RC is issued. Verify the total on your own Vahan payment screen before you pay, because that screen is the final figure for your vehicle.

What adding hypothecation means

Hypothecation records a financier's interest in a vehicle that is under a hire-purchase, lease or hypothecation agreement, under Section 51 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988. You stay the registered owner and you keep possession of the vehicle. The RC simply gains an entry naming the financier and the agreement, so anyone checking the record, a buyer, an insurer or the RTO, can see that the vehicle is financed. It must be removed separately when the loan ends, using Form 35: see our hypothecation removal guide.

Form 34 is the prescribed form for this entry, under Rule 60 of the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989. Its full title is "Application for making an entry of an agreement of hire-purchase, lease or hypothecation subsequent to registration". The key word is subsequent: Form 34 is for an agreement made after registration. If the loan was part of the original purchase, the dealer and financier usually arrange the entry at registration itself, and you should check the RC you received rather than filing Form 34 again.

When you need Form 34

  • You buy a vehicle outright, register it in your name, and later take a loan against it.
  • You refinance an existing vehicle with a new financier, so a new agreement must be recorded.
  • You take a loan on a used vehicle you already own, for example a loan against the vehicle, and the financier asks for the entry on the RC.
  • A lease or hire-purchase agreement starts after the vehicle is already on the road.

If you are changing financiers, termination of the old agreement and addition of the new one are separate services, Form 35 and Form 34. Confirm the order the portal asks for before you treat the switch as complete.

Steps: add hypothecation on Vahan

  1. Confirm the mobile number on the record. Vahan sends an OTP to the mobile number linked to the vehicle. If that number is no longer yours, update it first: see our mobile number update guide.
  2. Open Vahan Vehicle Related Services. Go to parivahan.gov.in, choose Vehicle Related Services, select Kerala, and enter your registration number.
  3. Verify the vehicle. Enter the last five digits of the chassis number when asked, generate the OTP, and submit it.
  4. Choose Hypothecation Addition. Select it from the Vehicle Related Services list and submit the service request.
  5. Enter the financier details. Fill in the financier name, address and agreement or loan account number exactly as in the finance documents.
  6. Enter insurance details where asked. Submit the current insurance information through the portal.
  7. Upload the supporting documents. Use the upload option for the documents Vahan lists for your application, and check the file format and size limits shown on screen.
  8. Check for an appointment. Some applications are processed without a visit; others generate an appointment or an instruction to visit the registering authority with originals. Follow the instruction on your application, not a general claim that the service is fully online.
  9. Pay the fee. Open the fee details, check the total against the table above, and pay through the portal gateway.
  10. Save the receipt and application number. Keep both until the updated RC is in hand, and use the application number to track the status on Vahan.

What changes after approval

Once the authority records the entry, download a fresh RC copy from DigiLocker or mParivahan and check the financier spelling, the agreement number, and that no other RC detail has changed. Raise any error with the RTO promptly, with the approval and receipt in hand.

An active entry also changes a future sale: transfer needs the financier's involvement, and the transfer form carries a financier consent section. A buyer should always check the RC for a financier entry before paying: see our RC transfer guide.

Common problems and fixes

  • OTP never arrives: the mobile number on the vehicle record is usually outdated or wrong. Update it first, then retry.
  • Vehicle details do not match: re-enter the registration number and the last five chassis digits exactly as on the RC. A single wrong digit blocks verification.
  • Financier details differ from the agreement: get a corrected Form 34 or letter from the financier. Do not edit the financier's name to fit the portal.
  • Payment pending: check the payment status and the application status before paying again, so you do not pay twice for one application.
  • Service not shown for your vehicle: some records, especially older paper records that are not fully digitised, are not eligible for the online route. In that case the RTO counter route, with Form 34 and originals, is the fallback.

FAQs

Does the financier become the owner of my vehicle?

No. You remain the registered owner. Hypothecation records the financier's interest; it does not transfer ownership.

What is the difference between Form 34 and Form 35?

Form 34 adds a hire-purchase, lease or hypothecation agreement to the RC. Form 35 terminates that agreement after it ends. They are opposite ends of the same lifecycle.

Can I add hypothecation online in Kerala?

Hypothecation Addition is a Vahan Vehicle Related Service, so the application starts online. Whether your application finishes without a visit depends on your vehicle record and the instructions Vahan shows for it.

Is there a fixed Kerala timeline for approval?

No fixed Kerala processing timeline for this service could be verified on an official page at research time, so none is quoted here. Track your application number on Vahan, and follow any appointment or visit instruction the portal gives.

Can I sell the vehicle while hypothecation is active?

Only with the financier's involvement, since the transfer process includes a financier consent step. Close or transfer the finance arrangement first, or complete the sale through the route the financier and the RTO confirm.
