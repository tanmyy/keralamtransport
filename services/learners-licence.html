Learner's Licence in Kerala

Online + test at RTO

Fee: see the fee table below. Time: varies by RTO.

Applicants report a 20-question test needing 12 correct answers, but the format can vary. Practise with the Parivahan question bank. Originals and copies may both be asked for.

Steps

  1. Apply for the learner's licence on Parivahan
  2. Upload documents and pay the fee online
  3. Book a test slot
  4. Attend the computer-based rules test at your RTO
  5. Download the learner's licence after passing

Documents

Accepted examples: passport, voter ID, Aadhaar, electricity or telephone bill, bank passbook, registered rent agreement.
Recent photo with a plain white background, as per the portal's upload rules.

Fee table

Application feeOfficial fee: Rs 150 (amounts may change without notice)
Test feeOfficial fee: Rs 50 (amounts may change without notice)
Learner renewal, if neededOfficial fee: to be confirmed on parivahan.gov.in (some applicants report ~Rs 300 - amounts may vary and change without notice)
Re-test, if neededOfficial fee: to be confirmed on parivahan.gov.in (some applicants report ~Rs 600 - amounts may vary and change without notice)

Common mistakes that get applications rejected

  • Name or date of birth spelled differently across documents.
  • Blurred or expired uploads.
  • Paying before checking the details on screen.
What is the fee for Learner's Licence in Kerala?

The official fee is listed on Parivahan and changes without notice. Check it there before you pay.
