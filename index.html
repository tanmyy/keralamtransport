Questions people ask before applying

Are the fees on this site final?

No. Each fee is shown as the official amount from Parivahan, and amounts change without notice. Where a fee is not yet confirmed we say so instead of guessing.

Is Keralam Transport a government site?

No. It is an independent guide and is not affiliated with Kerala MVD. Every application is filed on parivahan.gov.in.

How hard is the Kerala driving test?

Kerala's driving test is India's toughest by reputation, and the ground test rewards practice. Book enough lessons before your slot.

Do I need to visit the RTO?

Many steps are online, but some services still need an in-person visit for a test or verification. Each service page shows an online or offline badge.
